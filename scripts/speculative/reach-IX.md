BODY:
<p>The buildings are the hardware. Cheyenne Mountain sits behind 25-ton blast doors, the only Defense facility certified to survive a high-altitude electromagnetic pulse. In 2015 they went back inside the mountain. Greek Island ran a full nuclear bunker for Congress beneath a luxury resort for 30 years, and no one outside the program knew. The one that was exposed is the one we know about.</p>
<p>The software is the shadow. Devolution requires every covered federal organisation to keep a pre-designated second staff, at a separate site, standing by to assume its statutory authority - up within 12 hours, sustained for 30 days. The operative policy, PPD-40, has never been published. The list of who sits in which tier is Annex A, inside the classified directive. As of 2017, 56 Presidential Emergency Action Documents stood pre-written and pre-cleared, awaiting only a signature. None has ever been declassified or leaked, and no congressional committee reviews them. Their declassified ancestors authorised detention of "dangerous persons", suspension of habeas corpus, martial law and censorship of news.</p>
<p>And when a cleared member of the House Homeland Security Committee asked in 2007 to read the operative plan, he was refused, and said so on the floor. The legislature was shut out of the plan for keeping the government going. That is the design, not the leak in it. The surface manages the population; the subsurface provides continuity for the managing class.</p>
<p>The principle runs below every flag. Dimona hid a weapons facility beneath a reactor until one technician broke the silence and lost his freedom for it, and the arsenal it builds officially does not exist. Controlled access, deniable function, a parallel layer the public pays for and is not allowed to read.</p>
