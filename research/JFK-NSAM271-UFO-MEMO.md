## Verdict, claim by claim

| # | Claim | Tier | The record |
|---|-------|------|------------|
| 1 | NSAM 271 issued 12 Nov 1963 on Soviet space cooperation | **Documented** | FRUS 1961–63 vol. XXV, doc. 410, read in full: "Cooperation with the USSR on Outer Space Matters", JFK to NASA Administrator Webb. |
| 2 | "JFK reaches agreement with Khrushchev to share UFO files and run joint space missions" | **Contradicted by the document it cites** | NSAM 271 asks Webb to *develop proposals* "with a view to their possible discussion with the Soviet Union", following JFK's 20 Sept 1963 UN offer of "a joint expedition to the Moon". It is an internal directive. It records no agreement, and **it says nothing about UFOs**. |
| 2a | Khrushchev was about to accept | **Attributed** | Sergei Khrushchev (SpaceDaily, 2 Oct 1997) says his father "reversed himself in early November, 1963" and decided to accept the joint lunar offer. ## Symmetry: what is real

- Ten days before Dallas, Kennedy formally ordered NASA to prepare cooperation proposals with the
  USSR, lunar landing included (NSAM 271), after offering a joint Moon expedition at the UN on
  20 Sept 1963. That is documented, and it is the post's strongest true sentence.
- NSAM 271 itself praises "the channel of contact developed by Dr. Dryden between NASA and the Soviet
  Academy of Sciences". Limited cooperation already existed.
- Sergei Khrushchev's account is the best support for the "joint missions" half. It stays attributed.
