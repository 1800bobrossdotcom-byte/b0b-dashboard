### 2.1 Christine on Chiliad, to Ghislaine, 2003 - documented and EFTA-citable

**`vol00009-efta00578914`, 16 June 2003, Christine Maxwell to Ghislaine Maxwell**, subject line
carrying *"STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION"*:

> *"Much as I would like to wave my magic wand and get Chiliad shares to be instantly MONEY, it
> will still be a while yet before that event takes place... **I still have to get my control of
> the company back.** In the meantime, I am helping the Institute of scientific simulation - ISS
> as a Senior Partner - I thought I would ask you whether you would be interested in a small
> investment opportunity in ISS..."*

**Tier: documented.** **Establishes:** that in 2003 Christine Maxwell was discussing Chiliad
equity with her sister, considered her own control of the company to have been lost and to need
recovering, and was soliciting Ghislaine into a separate venture. **Does not establish:** anything
about what Chiliad's software was used for or by whom.

### 2.2 The 2008 share thread - the best material, and it is a LEAD not a citation

A May-June 2008 exchange runs: Christine ([redacted]@gmail.com, signing *"Christine
[Malina]-Maxwell"*) writes to Ghislaine under the subject **"re Consideration of acquiring some of
my chiliad shares - STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL"**. Ghislaine forwards it to Epstein
(`jeeproject@yahoo.com`): *"What do you think of this??"* and, on 1 June, *"Do you think I should
buy some shares?"* Epstein's reply asks the operator's question: **"how do they get money to deal
wiht the projected loss."** Ghislaine: *"I'll ask."* A further forward carries *"Here is
christines rather long convoluted reply.. re comments on 2007 financials... - Chiliad."*

**Correcting my own first reading, recorded rather than repaired:** I initially read *"my chiliad
shares"* as Epstein's. It is Christine's. **The accurate statement is that Christine offered her
own Chiliad stock to Ghislaine, and that Ghislaine took Epstein's advice on whether to buy it.**
Epstein as the financial adviser on the transaction, not the shareholder.

**Every document in this thread is currently recoverable only under a hex32 id. Under the standing
rule a hex32 is a research lead and never a citation. This cannot be published until an
EFTA-numbered copy is found.** That search is the single highest-value follow-up in this file, and
it is the same failure pattern as the January 2006 Cantor document from the 9/11 sweep.

### 2.6 The 2010 roster - documented, ambiguous, and it must stay ambiguous

**`EFTA01828294`, 8 December 2010.** Epstein, in a thread with Michael Keesling about a payment:

> *"i assume this 25k was not a few thousand that you are referring to - Al Seckel (not paid),
> **Isabel Maxwell (not paid), Christine Maxwell (not paid)**, Michael Keesling ($25,000), fee
> includes all charges for domains, hosting, and the Phillipine group, and web designers, etc. on
> his side. Stephanie Horenstein ($2500), **Hackers ($2500)**"*

A related message, `vol00009-efta00754633` of 20 October 2010, discusses wiring Keesling and
describes *"the underlying work that is involved in providing a 'steady' fix to such a problem, as
opposed to some cheap 'slip-shot' and 'one-shot' approach."*

**Tier: documented as to the existence and wording of the roster. Nothing beyond that.**
**What it establishes:** that both sisters' names appear on an Epstein-side list of participants
in a project involving domains, hosting, web designers and a line item reading "Hackers," and that
both are marked "(not paid)." **What it does not establish:** what the project was, what either
sister did or knew, or what "(not paid)" means - it is equally consistent with *owed and not yet
paid* and with *no payment due because no work was done*. **The ambiguity is the finding and it
must travel with the quote.** Nothing here characterises either person, and the living-person
floor governs: documented appearance on a list is not participation, and participation would not
be conduct.
