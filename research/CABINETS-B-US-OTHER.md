Internal research (`research/` is never served). Run 29 Oct 2026, beat B of the cabinets sweep. Beat A (Trump cabinet) is separate; where a person sits in both (Barr, Acosta) only the part that belongs here is recorded.

**Rules applied.** Every fetch through `scripts/spider/spider.py` (ledgered). Living-person floor: only documented contact, the person's own statement and the office-holder's official acts are recorded; no living, uncharged person is asserted or implied to be a criminal or an intelligence asset, and documented contact is never an operational tie. No survivor is named (the civil plaintiff whose 2016 deposition names Richardson is "the plaintiff" below; the page already names her, that is the orchestrator's call). No jmail. No FD-302, victim-interview or protective-order document was opened. No redaction de-anonymised. No house numbers. `site/` and `CLAUDE.md` untouched; nothing committed.

**Tiers used.** documented (a primary document read, or the person's own admission) / attributed (a secondary report of a document or claim not opened) / testimony / contested / unsupported. "Contact" is not "tie". Rung 4 and 5 are not claimed for anyone here.

**Quote check.** 142 quotations in the Q section, `vq.py --batch`: **142 checked, 0 not OK.** Not Q-checkable (recorded as method notes, not as quotes): the Obama visitor-record zips (binary), the Gonzales letter PDF (scanned, no text layer; its key sentence is quoted from the Committee's own release page instead), and the one OCR-garbled word in the Reagan Library file (see Reagan).

## 0. THE FINDINGS IN ONE PLACE

1. **Documented, strongest and new to the page:** Robert Maxwell's contact with the Reagan White House is a primary-source file, not a rumour. The Reagan Library folder "Maxwell, Robert" (WHORM Alpha File) holds: a 2 May 1988 letter from the Chief of Staff's personal assistant declining Mrs. Maxwell's invitation to Pergamon Press's 40th-anniversary dinner (10 June 1988); a June 1988 staff request for a presidential birthday greeting to Maxwell, routed through Hill & Knowlton's Robert Keith Gray and marked for hand delivery by Fred Ryan; Maxwell's 13 July 1988 thank-you letter; and a telegram thanking the President for a gift to the Sunday Mirror's Ethiopian-appeal auction. **Contact by correspondence and invitation; no meeting with a cabinet officer is recorded in it.** The page has none of it.
2. **John Tower–Maxwell is not documented.** It exists as (a) Gordon Thomas and Martin Dillon's 2002 book, relayed by Wikipedia with "citation needed", (b) a 1990s Consortium News piece saying Tower was on Maxwell's Pergamon-Brassey payroll at his death, and (c) Ari Ben-Menashe, whom that same piece reports was met with "widespread denials and derision". No filing, hearing record, letter or payroll record was found. Tier: attributed, single chain, unsupported as to anything Maxwell directed. Tower was never a cabinet officer (the Senate rejected him, 47–53, March 1989).
3. **Clinton:** documented contact in office is **Epstein and Maxwell at a 29 Sept 1993 White House donors' reception** (Clinton Library photographs, per Snopes quoting the Library) and a $10,000 White House Historical Association gift (Daily Beast, citing the Association's letter). The "17 visits" are logged visits, mostly to a staff aide; the Daily Mail/Daily Beast say it is unclear whether Epstein saw Clinton on them. **Post-presidency:** 2002–03 flights are documented; the **count is disputed on its face** (Clinton's office: four trips; Fox 2016 reading FAA logs: at least 26; Comer 2026: at least 27). The island: no document read places Clinton there; both Epstein (2011 email, relayed) and Maxwell (DOJ interview) say he never went.
4. **Summers** is the best-documented cabinet-level contact: flight log **19 Sept 1998 while Deputy Treasury Secretary** (The Nation, citing the logs), the Harvard years (Epstein's programme money negotiated with Summers, 2003), a **Dec 2005 under-a-day island visit on his honeymoon, his spokesman's admission**, flight records from the Maxwell trial showing at least four flights (Wikipedia), and 2013–19 correspondence and meetings, ending 5 July 2019. His statement: "I am deeply ashamed…". No dated contact **inside** his Treasury Secretary (1999–2001) or NEC (2009–10) tenures was found.
5. **Ruemmler** now has a **sworn-equivalent primary: the House Oversight transcript of 15 July 2026** (released 29 July). Her account: first contact July 2014, **after** she left the White House (May 2014), by Epstein's cold call. Nothing in the Obama White House years is documented. The page's heaviest correspondent is a **post-office** relationship.
6. **Burns:** the page says "documented three Epstein meetings". The record is **three scheduled in Epstein's calendar (WSJ, 2023), two acknowledged by his own spokesperson (2026), plus a documented email exchange on his last day as Deputy Secretary, 31 Oct 2014.** The 2023 CIA line ("met … once briefly") is contradicted by the 2026 messages. Page needs the correction.
7. **Richardson is not on the page at all** (the five grep hits are *US v. Richardson* and Joint Base Elmendorf-Richardson). He is the one clear omission: DOJ-released emails show at least nine meetings arranged after Epstein's 2008 conviction, a Nov 2010 island visit coordinated by his aide, a Jan 2011 helicopter manifest line, and **Epstein asking him in Nov–Dec 2010 to approach Florida's governor about clemency** (Crist denies any contact). Richardson's cabinet years (UN ambassador 1997–98, Energy 1998–2001) show **no** documented contact; the first records are 2002 on.
8. **Official acts on the matter in office are thin and mostly institutional.** Bush 43: the NPA (24 Sept 2007) was signed a week after AG Gonzales left; the Criminal Division and the Deputy AG's office reviewed it and the DAG declined to intervene on 23 June 2008 (OPR); Gonzales's 2025 letter: "no present memory". Obama: the SDFL US Attorney's Office litigated the CVRA case and opposed unsealing; Holder, by his lawyers, says the notoriety came after his tenure; Lynch's response not found. Biden: Garland wrote that he recalls no status update. **No cabinet officer in any administration here is documented as personally acting on the Epstein matter.**
9. **Obama White House visitor records (WAVES, Sept 2009–2016), searched locally: zero "Maxwell, Ghislaine"; five "Epstein, Jeff*" records, four of them tour-group/other middle initials; one "EPSTEIN, JEFFREY E" appointment on 10 Feb 2011 (visitee a staff member, three people, no description) that is a name-and-initial match only.** Epstein's middle name is Edward. Not attributable; this is the page's own naming-error trap. Follow-up named in §10.
10. **Nulls are printed in §8.** Carter, Bush 41 (Epstein), Rubin, Albright, Greenspan, Cheney/Rumsfeld/Powell/Rice, Panetta/Carter/Yellen/Geithner and Biden-cabinet names beyond Burns and Garland: nothing documented. The search layer for most of these was web search, not a full-text index of the 3.5M-page DOJ library (its search endpoint is an Akamai decoy), so those nulls are **floors**.
