## 2. ISRAEL - the famous pattern is nearly worthless, and the real link is elsewhere

### 2.1 The 8200 founder pipeline collapses in the filing layer

The canonical story is that Unit 8200 alumni founded Israel's cyber industry. **It is real, it is
self-published, and as evidence it is close to worthless** - and in the filings it barely exists.

**EDGAR full-text, 2001 to 2026, yields exactly one major Israeli cyber firm stating an
executive's 8200 command:** Check Point's 20-F on CEO Nadav Zafrir - *"Commander of Unit 8200...
established the IDF Cyber Command"* - who also co-founded Team8. Against that:

- **"8200" appears zero times** in Palo Alto Networks' FY2025 proxy and FY2026 10-K, including in
  Nir Zuk's retirement disclosure.
- **Zero times** in Cellebrite's 20-F.
- **Zero times** for Gil Shwed in any Check Point filing.

**The canonical founder table is reporting, not document.** And the counter-argument is decisive
on its own: Israel has near-universal conscription and 8200 is a very large technical unit, so an
8200 alumnus founding a company is **the expected output of a national talent pipeline, not
evidence of an operation.** That is the anti-map guardrail applied to the most impressive-looking
pattern in the whole subject, and the pattern does not survive it.

### 2.2 What actually binds these firms to the state: the export licence

**This is the documented state link, it is structural rather than personal, and the report has
zero mentions of it.**

Cellebrite's own 20-F sets out the regime: the **Defence Export Control Agency (DECA)** inside the
Ministry of Defence, the **Defense Export Control Law 5767-2007**, the dual-use order 5766-2006,
and Israel's non-membership of Wassenaar despite adopting its list.

**The sharpest illustration:** NSO's US buyers could not purchase their way out of it. The sale
required DECA approval, and NSO's own spokesman said it *"does not mean the company is moving out
of Israeli regulatory or operational control."*

**New and post-cutoff:** the Defence Minister signed an order on **18 November 2025** repealing the
encryption regime, effective **18 March 2026**.

## 3. SOVEREIGN AND ROYAL CAPITAL - the anti-map test, and it returns a clean answer

### 3.1 "The fund is the state" is true for some and false for others

**True:** **PIF** - the Crown Prince and Prime Minister chairs it, *and* chairs the body PIF
reports to. **GIC** - chairman is the Senior Minister, deputy chairman the sitting Prime Minister
and Finance Minister, from GIC's own page.

**False:** **QIA** (chair moved to the Central Bank Governor), **Temasek**, **KIA**, **NBIM**.

**ADIA** is the sharpest adjacency: chaired by the **National Security Adviser and Deputy Ruler of
Abu Dhabi** - a different office from head of government, and the same person chairs G42 and MGX.

**A blanket "sovereign funds are their states" claim is false on the record. State it fund by
fund or not at all.**

### 3.2 Documented positions

- **PIF's 13F, 30 June 2026**: exactly five US positions - **SpaceX 154,146,835 shares,
  $26.34bn**, EA $5.09bn, Uber $5.26bn, Lucid $1.18bn, Claritev. SpaceX is now a Nasdaq
  registrant; PIF's stake is **2.00%** of Class A shares against Musk's reported 48.4%.
- **Norges/NBIM**: 24 defence, cyber and surveillance names with exact share counts - Palantir
  $3.63bn, Booz Allen $120m, CACI, Leidos, SAIC, Check Point, Planet Labs, Leonardo DRS - against
  a Ministry of Finance mandate **barring more than 10% of voting shares in any company.**

### 3.3 The test result, and it is the finding

**Two direction instruments exist, and both are ordinary, publicly filed corporate-control
documents:**

- **Mubadala / GlobalFoundries** (20-F FY2025): beneficial ownership *"approximately 81%"*, a
  shareholder's agreement carrying consent rights, and the right to **nominate a majority of the
  board** - five named designees.
- **PIF / Lucid** (DEF 14A, 23 April 2026): Ayar controls **over 50% of voting power** and
  nominates **five of nine** directors under an Investor Rights Agreement of 22 February 2021.
  PIF's Deputy Governor chairs the board.

**There is no golden share anywhere in the set.** And the only *security* instruments found run
**the other way** - constraining the foreign owner rather than empowering it: SpaceX's FOCI
mitigation with the Department of War, GlobalFoundries' FOCI agreements tied to its clearance and
its **$1.5bn CHIPS Direct Funding Agreement**, and the BIS-approved Regulated Technology
Environment for G42.

**So: where sovereign control of a strategic company exists, it is exercised through published
corporate documents, and the state security apparatus is documented constraining it.** That is a
more interesting and far more defensible finding than covert direction, and it is checkable line
by line.

## 4. THE BANKING FAMILIES - tested to destruction, both directions

### 4.1 What died, on arithmetic and public law

- **"$500 trillion."** Exceeds all household wealth on Earth - UBS Global Wealth Report 2025 puts
  the total at **$471tn**. Killed without touching the family.
- **"The Fed is privately owned by the families."** **12 U.S.C. §287** bars transfer or
  hypothecation of Reserve Bank stock. **No family can hold it.**
- ***Lewis v. United States*, 680 F.2d 1239**, the case always cited for it, is **an FTCA
  car-accident holding**, and the same opinion calls Reserve Banks *"federal instrumentalities for
  some purposes."*
- **"They own most central banks."** Private shareholding in the **BIS was abolished** - an
  Extraordinary General Meeting of **8 January 2001** compulsorily repurchased all **72,648**
  privately held shares at **CHF 16,000**, arbitrated at The Hague.
- **The Warburg conflation.** **Warburg Pincus** was acquired by **Lionel I. Pincus & Co. in
  1966** and has been a private partnership since. **S.G. Warburg** went to Swiss Bank Corporation
  in 1995 and into UBS in 1998. **Only M.M.Warburg is family-held** - and it earned **one million
  euros in 2024 at a 112.4% cost-income ratio** and failed to find a buyer.

### 4.2 What survived, and it is worth having

- **Rothschild & Co family control is real and documented**: Concordia took it private at
  **€38.60 a share, 95.65% of capital.** Its size, from audited accounts to 31 December 2025:
  **total assets €19.99bn**, net banking income €2.99bn, **4,787 full-time equivalents.** The
  comparison against the global top ten by assets is the finding. *Counter-record, and it is real:
  the advisory franchise ranks near the top of global M&A league tables by deal count despite the
  small balance sheet, so "they are tiny therefore irrelevant" runs the symmetry rule the other
  way.*
- **M.M.Warburg's cum-ex record is adverse and docketed**: LG Bonn 62 KLs 1/19,
  **€176,574,602 forfeiture**, upheld by the Bundesgerichtshof, 1 StR 519/20.
- **The 19th-century five-house structure was real** - and **three of five are gone**: Naples 1863,
  Frankfurt 1901, and **Vienna seized after the Anschluss of 13 March 1938** and sold under duress.
  **That last fact cuts directly against the use this material is usually put to.**
- **Wallenberg / Investor AB is the one fully documented case of concentrated family control of
  listed industry**, and it is entirely public: the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation holds
  **20.1% of capital carrying 43.0% of votes**, the foundations together **50.2% of votes**,
  through a one-vote / one-tenth-vote dual share class. Adjusted NAV **SEK 1,087bn**; portfolio
  companies employ over 500,000. **Where family control of industry actually exists at scale, it
  leaves a paper trail this thick. The absence of a comparable trail for the families the
  literature prefers is itself evidence.**
