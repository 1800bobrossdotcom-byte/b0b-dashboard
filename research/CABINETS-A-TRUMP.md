## 0. Method, and the limits of what a "null" means here

1. **The DOJ's own list is the spine.** DOJ's Section 3 report to Congress (14 Feb 2026, signed by AG Bondi and DAG Blanche) lists every "government official or politically exposed person" whose name "appears in the files released under the Act at least once". It says names "appear ... in a wide variety of contexts": some "had extensive direct email contact with Epstein", others are "mentioned only in a portion of a document (including press reporting) that on its face is unrelated". **So a name on the list is not a contact; and the list is not complete** ("Any omissions from the list are unintentional"). The proof that omission is real: **Tom Barrack is not on the list, although NBC says he is "mentioned hundreds of times in the files" and CBS reviewed 100+ of his texts and emails.** Absence from the list is therefore a weak null, not a clean one.
2. **The DOJ library cannot be searched from here** (`/epstein/search` answers with a decoy 200; `/search/` is disallowed by robots). I could read any EFTA document **by id**, and ids came from the Oversight transcripts, the earlier research files, and news that cites them. Contact found this way is a **floor**.
3. **Cabinet classification used.** Cabinet = department heads and the VP. Cabinet-level = chief of staff, UN ambassador, DNI, CIA, OMB, USTR, EPA etc. **Cabinet-adjacent (flagged, not counted)**: FBI director and deputy, deputy AG, special envoys, senior WH staff. **Steve Bannon**: not cabinet; classified "cabinet-level staff" only for Jan–Aug 2017, because the 28 Jan 2017 presidential memorandum made his post a regular attendee of the NSC Principals Committee, "a Cabinet-level senior inter-agency forum" (Wikipedia; secondary). Treat that as a classification, not a finding.
4. A Wikipedia body-text screen of ~70 cabinet-level biographies for the word "Epstein" was run and **discarded**: it returned 0 even for Kushner and the Bondi-adjacent figures, so it measures nothing.
5. Interested-speaker discount applies to every subject's own account **and** to Maxwell's (convicted, clemency-dependent) and to Epstein's emails (he boasts, he tracks, he flatters).

## 5. Contested lines, and lines on the page that this beat contradicts or cannot support

1. **Lutnick dinner (VIII cabinet map; XXI).** The page says "Epstein contributed to Lutnick dinner 2018." **EFTA02229607 (9 Nov 2017)**: the UJA Wall Street Dinner honouring Lutnick was **11 Dec 2017**; the solicitation asked about "a table for 10" ($50,000); **Epstein's reply: "no, tell lutnik he can fill them"**; the organiser: "Amanda will give the table to Howard". Whether Epstein gave something smaller, or took a journal page or "Roll of Honor" line, is **not shown in this document**. CNN (via Wikipedia) reports a $50,000 donation, unread at source. Fix to "solicited in Nov 2017; his reply and what he paid are not established in the document read".
2. **Lutnick's own two accounts.** Oct 2025: "never in the room with him socially, for business or even philanthropy". May 2026: three in-person occasions, one of them a "social lunch". Transcript: asked whether the earlier line was misleading, "It was not misleading, no." and "'I' refers to Howard Lutnick. 'We' refers to my wife and myself together." Documented inconsistency between statements; the Democrats' letter calls it "lied" (a characterisation). Printed both ways.
3. **Barr and recusal.** Reporting on 8–9 Jul 2019 said he had recused from the review of the 2008 deal but not the SDNY case; in 2025 he said, of the NPA review, "I just was never involved in that", and of the SDNY case, "I did not recuse myself." Not contradictory on its face, but the two accounts differ in what they call a recusal. Contemporaneous source (Newsweek via Wikipedia) not read.
4. **"Acosta was told Epstein belonged to intelligence."** One anonymous source (Daily Beast, 2019; read via Wikipedia). Acosta denied it in the OPR process (per Wikipedia); the OPR executive summary fetched here contains no such passage. **Attributed, single-source.**
5. **"Epstein bragged Bill Barr was in charge."** Michael Wolff's book *Too Famous* (2021), via the Daily Beast: Epstein to Ehud Barak, "At the moment, Bill Barr is in charge." Secondhand, an Epstein boast to a third party, undated. **Attributed; no document.**
6. **Bondi's firing.** Reasons reported as failed prosecutions of Trump's opponents plus Epstein handling (NBC, anonymous officials); Trump called it a private-sector move. **Contested.**
7. **Page lines in the same subsections, observed but not mine:** the Bessent block (V `iv-the-bessent-adjacency…`) contains "none of which is alleged or charged" and "No personal Epstein link ... (unsupported)" reassurance sentences of the §2 class; the cabinet map's "$25,000 Trump Foundation donation received before she declined to investigate Trump University fraud" is not an Epstein item and was not checked here.
