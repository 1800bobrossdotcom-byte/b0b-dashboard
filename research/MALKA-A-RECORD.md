**Summary.** Identity confirmed across filings: Meyer "Micky" Malka (MercadoLibre's 2013 8-K gives "Malka Rais"; SEC filer CIK 0001571355), founder and managing partner of Ribbit Capital (Palo Alto; SEC-registered adviser, CRD 165308).
- **Claim 1, "appointed to the board of Apollo Global Wealth": true in substance, wrong in name and tense.** Apollo's own 8-K of **20 Oct 2026** says the board of **Apollo Global Management, Inc.** appointed him an independent director, **effective 1 Jan 2027**. "Apollo Global Wealth" is not a board; it is the label Apollo uses for its individual-investor business. **There is no overlap with Leon Black:** Black left that board on 21 Mar 2021, nearly six years before Malka's seat takes effect. No document read ties Malka to Black.

In IRS data shown by ProPublica, Malka is listed as **Secretary/director**. **Becky Kleiner is President/CEO/director**, and the press names Phil Mohun as executive director. The press says Malka and Kleiner gave its $25 million founding grant. NODE has nothing to do with Node.js, OpenJS or a crypto "node" entity.
