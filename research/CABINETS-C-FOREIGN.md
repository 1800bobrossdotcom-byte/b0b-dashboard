## 0. What the record says in six lines

1. **The pattern is of consequences, not of findings.** Six cabinet-level or head-of-government figures from five countries have had a documented official consequence since 30 Jan 2026: Mandelson (UK) dismissed in 2025 and arrested in 2026; Jagland (Norway) formal suspect; Lang (France) under preliminary investigation; Lajčák (Slovakia) resigned as adviser; Brende (Norway) resigned as WEF head; Waheed (Maldives) resigned an envoy post. No conviction in the sources read. Barak (Israel) has no official consequence found.
2. **The UK has the only cabinet-level record in the government's own hand.** The Cabinet Office's due-diligence note (Humble Address return, 11 Mar 2026) and the Commons record carry the government's account; a Blair meeting is documented by a 14 May 2002 Downing Street briefing memo and Mandelson's email to Blair's chief of staff, reported by the BBC and relayed by Anadolu.
3. **Norway is the most complete official response**: Økokrim investigation (5 Feb), Council of Europe immunity waiver (11 Feb), searches (12 Feb), a joint investigation team with France (27 Apr), a Storting commission (final report due 31 Jan 2028) and open hearings of eleven current and former ministers (30 Sept – 1 Oct).
4. **Poland and the Maldives are the "other" cases.** Poland's prime minister set up an analytical team (3 Feb); the Maldives' ex-president resigned a special-envoy post (3 Feb).
5. **Robert Maxwell's record with heads of government is thin and mostly secondary.** What is primary: a TNA file titled "Prime Minister's meetings with Robert Maxwell" (PREM 19/3492, 1987–91, unread) and Pergamon's publication of Brezhnev, Gorbachev and Honecker titles. Shamir's attendance at the funeral is documented (JTA, already on the page).
6. **A shared counterpart is not a chain.** Three ministers' correspondence touches the Russian foreign minister (Barak 2015, Jagland 2018, Lajčák 2018). Lavrov is the Russian foreign minister of the whole period; anyone who deals with Russia meets him. Anti-map guardrail applies (§5).
