## Claim by claim

| Claim | Grade |
|---|---|
| Iran is "the primary source of uncovered liquidity for the shadow banking system" | **Unsupported, and inverted by the record.** "Shadow banking" in the documents is Iran's own sanctions-evasion network, a channel for Iran to get foreign currency, not a source of liquidity to London. Treasury, Aug 2026: "Iran is desperate for foreign currency". FinCEN measured ~$9bn of potential Iranian shadow-banking activity through US correspondent accounts in 2024: the scale of an evasion network, not a funding source for a financial centre. |
| "London is the hub" | **Unsupported.** FinCEN names the UAE, Hong Kong and Singapore as the hubs; Treasury's 2026 actions name Dubai exchange houses and Hong Kong fronts. London does not appear in either text read. |
| IRGC energy credits traded for vostro credits "in mid-sized Omani banks" | **Unsupported.** No document read names an Omani bank. Oman is on the page 42 times, as the mediator. |
| Bundled into "synthetic bonds" that reach London repo desks as "high-quality collateral" | **Unsupported, and the mechanism fails.** No instrument, issuer, ISIN or counterparty is named. The Bank of England publishes its eligible collateral by security and asks ABS issuers for loan-level transparency; "dark paper" is what that regime is built to exclude. "High-quality" is a regulatory class, not an adjective. |
| City of London leverage ratios "would collapse" without it | **Unsupported.** No figure, no source. |
| Two elite "factions" (central bankers vs Silicon Valley) fighting through "accidents", crashes and security breaches | **Refused.** Unfalsifiable (every accident fits), anti-map (a shared year is not a chain), and the owner is moved from named governments to an unnamed class: the families trope with new labels, which the Substack frame states outright. | "There's no blockade" | **Fails on the dates.** Written 16 Mar 2026; the US blockade of Iranian ports ran from 13 Apr to 18 June and again from 14 July (CENTCOM's count, on the page in XV: 115 vessels redirected by 23 Sept, 138 by 17 Oct). |
| "Sanctions aren't walls; they're tolls … only the top 5 banks" | **Unsupported as stated.** That compliance costs concentrate correspondent banking is an argued thesis in the de-risking literature, not read here; "top 5" has no source. |
| "Seizing Iran's central bank" to wipe out "the last off-network gold reserves" | **Unsupported.** No seizure of Iran's central bank or its gold is reported in anything read. |
| "A 30% liquidity haircut in under 48 hours", visible in the gold-to-oil ratio | **Unfalsifiable as written.** "Liquidity haircut" for a city is not a measured quantity; no baseline, no instrument. The loop it describes is the network the US has been attacking all year (below), and no such event is reported. |
| "Escalation will likely involve cutting undersea cables" | **A forecast, not a finding.** Cable damage in wartime is a known risk; nothing here ties it to this thesis. |
