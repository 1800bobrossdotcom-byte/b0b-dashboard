## 0. What the staff report is, and what it is not

- **Document:** unsigned, ~50–53 pp., written by Democratic staff for Ranking Member Raskin; released 26 Oct 2026, eight days before the 3 Nov midterms. Title: "'We Have Certain Things in Common, Jeffrey': The Case Against Donald Trump Emerging in the Epstein Files and DOJ's Desperate Efforts to Cover it Up." PDF: `democrats-judiciary.house.gov/.../2026-10.hjc-dems-staff-report-epstein-and-trump.pdf` (text layer, 2,227 lines: read the executive summary, the Keul/Johnson/Farmer/Kate sections selectively, and pp.36–52 in full; the rest skimmed by search for Giuffre, withdrawal, denial and credibility terms).
- **Status:** a minority staff product, "rather than findings by the full House Judiciary Committee or a court" (Newsweek). MS NOW: the Judiciary Committee "has not been the principal congressional committee investigating Epstein"; that is Oversight.
- **Its conclusion** (executive summary): Trump "was an active participant in Jeffrey Epstein's sexual abuse of young women during the mid-to-late 1990s"; Trump and Paolo Zampolli "may have been co-conspirators"; the evidence "indisputably meets and surpasses" the FBI standard for opening a full investigation. It also says DOJ obstructed, and Blanche's July 2025 Maxwell interview "potentially constituted witness tampering".
- **What it adds that is new** (MS NOW, Rubin): "at least one entirely new allegation ... it provides no direct evidence other than the woman's own conversations with Judiciary Committee staff." That is Beatrice Keul's rape account, given to staff over "several weeks". TIME: the other four direct allegations "had all been previously made public either through media interviews or FBI testimony released as part of the Epstein files."
- **The "11 women" arithmetic, from the report itself:** 5 direct accusers of rape/sexual assault in Epstein's company or presence (Keul, Stacey Williams, Jill Harth, "Katie Johnson", and one Jane Doe); 3 who describe being offered or presented (witness-trial "Jane", witness-trial "Kate", Maria Farmer); 3 who "have not come forward as survivors" (Lauren Petrella, Doe 5, Doe 6). Popular Info: Petrella "has never made accusations against Trump herself"; Does 5 and 6 "have not accused Trump of any wrongdoing." Farmer herself says (page, citing her) she has no evidence Trump committed a crime. So "11 women" is a count of women connected by the report to the Trump–Epstein orbit, not 11 accusers; the report's own press release says so.

**Tier for the report as a source:** the *document* is documented (it exists, as published). Its *characterisations* are contested. Its *quotations of DOJ documents* are checkable and several were checked here (§1.4).
