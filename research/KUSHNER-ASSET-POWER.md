## 2. Why a private citizen brokers state-level deals: the mechanism

**The power is the President's foreign-affairs power, exercised through a person he chooses.** No written appointment,
delegation, special-government-employee designation, oath, OGE 278 or ethics agreement for Kushner in 2025–26 has
been produced by anyone. CREW pleads that the White House and State, asked on 20 July 2026, said they hold none.

- **Two legal routes, both opened by the 20 Jan 2017 OLC opinion (read at source).**
  - The formal route: the White House hiring statute (3 U.S.C. §105(a)) overrides the anti-nepotism statute
    (5 U.S.C. §3110), on the premise that White House appointees stay bound by the conflict-of-interest laws
    (18 U.S.C. §§203–209). He used it in 2017–21 (Senior Adviser, disclosures filed, no salary).
  - The informal route: a President may consult a relative "on an unofficial, ad hoc basis" with no status. He has
    used this since 2025.
- **OLC's own 1977 test cuts the other way, but nobody has applied it to him.** An informal adviser who chairs meetings
  of agency staff and coordinates policy is doing government work and "should be formally appointed and take an oath
  of office". The same opinion says a missing appointment can be disregarded where it was left out to avoid the
  conflict laws. Applying it to Kushner is this file's analysis, not any court's or ethics office's.
- **What he holds in 2025–26, on the record:**
  - a title only spoken, never written: "we're making Jared an envoy also" (19 Feb 2026). "U.S. Special Envoy for
    Peace" appears in the compilation of presidential documents. **EO 14311 creates one Special Envoy for Peace
    Missions, and that is Witkoff,** who holds a "Non-Career" appointment (30 June 2025) and files disclosures;
  - seats on the Board of Peace Executive Board and the Gaza Executive Board (White House statement, 16 Jan 2026);
  - the White House's own description: a "private citizen" serving "on a volunteer basis" and paying his own expenses.
- **The laws that could constrain a private envoy do not reach him as he is being used:**
  - the **Logan Act** covers only acting "without authority of the United States". He acts with it. The Act has
    produced two indictments since 1799 (1803 and 1853) and no conviction (CRS);
  - **22 U.S.C. §2651a(j)** (Senate consent for powerful envoys) covers only envoys "at the Department of State";
  - the **special-government-employee and ethics statutes** start only when someone is "retained, designated,
    appointed, or employed". No document shows any of these for him.
  - **So the constraint is a lawsuit:** *CREW v. Kushner*, D.D.C. 1:26-cv-03580 (14 Oct 2026; complaint read). It has
    four counts: the Appointments Clause; the Ethics Act (an equitable claim and mandamus); and, in the alternative,
    private non-delegation. Its point is that whether he is an officer, an employee or a private citizen, the
    arrangement is unlawful. No answer or ruling was found.
- **The comparison that shows the gap:**
  - Witkoff does the same work with the office and the disclosures. Kushner has neither.
  - Giuliani in 2019 (House report 116-335) ran an "irregular channel" beside the official one. **Kushner is the
    official channel:** the complaint quotes Rubio telling the Senate that the envoys rely almost entirely on State
    Department staff.
- **Who objects:** only CREW and Democrats (statements, letters copied to OGE, the suit), and the letters are
  unanswered. No ethics office, inspector general or Republican-led committee has acted. The White House calls CREW
  "unserious, Trump-deranged". His lawyer says unnamed government ethics attorneys reviewed the arrangement, the suit
  has no merit and CREW lacks standing.
- **Not established:** that he is a special government employee; any Logan Act exposure in 2025–26; that Witkoff moved
  from State to the White House to avoid §2651a(j) (the dates are documented, the reason is not); that ambassadorships
  were left empty for him (AFSA, 20 Oct 2026: 93 of 196 posts vacant across the board); "no clearance" (there is only
  no public record).

**Plainly: he has the power because the President has it and lends it, and the structure that would make the
loan visible (an appointment, an oath, a disclosure) has not been used.** That absence is documented, and it is what
CREW is suing about. It is a gap in accountability on the public record. It is not evidence of a hidden sponsor.

## 3. "Epstein-esque continuance": the pattern does not carry over

Tested against **Section II's own four features** for Epstein's rung 4 (credentials that survived checking; two
clients paying on an unexplained scale; immunity for unnamed co-conspirators; elite access that survived a
conviction), its rivals B–D, and its rule that the conclusion "rises only on a document".

- **Parallel in shape only, each with a documented ordinary explanation:**
  - the 2019 clearance overrule (unnamed official; press identification);
  - Saudi PIF's $2bn over its own panel's reported objections (the money and fees documented, the objections
    attributed);
  - **new:** the 2017 Apollo ($184m) and Citigroup ($325m) loans to Kushner Companies after White House meetings. The
    House members' release of 9 Mar 2018 was read; the loans are attributed to the NYT. The lenders and his lawyer
    deny any link, and the lawyer says the White House counsel's review found "no issues". Meeting-then-loan is a
    shared timeline, not a chain (anti-map). The Apollo meetings were with Joshua Harris, not Leon Black.
- **Failed outright:**
  - **no immunity deal:** Mueller declined charges and gave his reasons;
  - **no conviction for access to survive:** Jared has none.
  - **His father's case runs opposite to Epstein's:** Charles was prosecuted to the top of his plea range. His access
    did survive the conviction (pardoned Dec 2020, ambassador to France 2025). That is the one documented "survival"
    in the family, and it is Charles's. Charles's adjudicated 2004 entrapment of a cooperating witness is **refused**
    as Jared's (naming error) and as tradecraft (anti-map).
- **The decisive failure is deniability.** Rung 4 is a role a sponsoring service "could disavow". **His role is
  avowed by the President in the White House's own published record.** Only its legal form is in dispute.
- **Kushner ↔ Epstein: below rung 1, attributed.** The only item found is a March 2013 New York Observer anniversary-gala
  invitation addressed to Epstein that carries "Jared Kushner, Publisher" (House Oversight estate release of
  12 Nov 2025, as reported by MEMO and MSNBC). Whether Epstein went is unknown, and no correspondence between them was
  found. **Null at outlet level only:** the 20,000-page estate set was not text-searched, jmail was not used (robots;
  the author's call pending) and the DOJ library was not searched.
- **Verdict:** the documents reach material ties to foreign **governments** (the PIF, UAE and Qatari money: rung 3),
  with no intelligence service in any of them. **Rival B (wealth plus family-political power, used openly) explains
  the record without strain.** What runs through both cases is concentrated power; the difference is visibility.
  Epstein's was power with no visible source. Kushner's source is on the record: the President, the family, and Gulf
  sovereign money. **That difference is the finding,** and it is the more useful one for a reader looking at power
  structures: the documented channel is open, legal by its defenders' account, and contested in court, not hidden.
