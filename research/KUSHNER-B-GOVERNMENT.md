# kushner — B: government roles, 2017–2026

**Summary.**

*Documented:*
- **The Mueller report's own findings on the 2016 contacts.**
  - Kushner met Kislyak at Trump Tower on 30 Nov 2016, and asked him to name someone who could speak for Putin.
  - He asked about secure facilities at the Russian embassy, and Kislyak rejected the idea.
  - He met VEB's Gorkov on 13 Dec 2016. The report "did not resolve" whether that meeting was diplomatic or business, and found no "substantive follow-up".
  - He attended the 9 June 2016 meeting and texted "waste of time".
  - **Charges:** the Office declined campaign-finance charges against "Trump Jr. or other campaign officials" for the June 9 events, and charged Kushner with nothing.
- **The 2019 House Oversight record on clearances.** The majority and minority memos of 1 Apr 2019 record that the personnel-security director overruled the career adjudicators' denial for "Senior White House Official 1". The memos do not name that official. NBC's sources (Jan 2019) say the official was Kushner.
- **His Abraham Accords role, in government transcripts:** the 11 Sept 2020 remarks, the 15 Sept pool roster ("Assistant to the President and Senior Advisor"), and Trump's Knesset speech (13 Oct 2025).
- **His 2025–26 envoy role.** The Daily Compilation of Presidential Documents gives no title on 13 Oct 2025 ("his son-in-law Jared C. Kushner"). On 19 Feb 2026 it reads **"U.S. Special Envoy for Peace Jared C. Kushner"**.
- **The 2 Dec 2025 Putin meeting:** about five hours, per the Kremlin aide via TASS and Interfax.

*Contested:*
- **His formal status.**
  - The government's own record titles him Special Envoy for Peace.
  - The White House says he "remains a private citizen" serving "on a volunteer basis" (Oct 2026).
  - His lawyer calls it "volunteer government service".
  - CREW sued on 14 Oct 2026, under the Appointments Clause and the Ethics Act.
  - **No special-government-employee designation or OGE filing was found in any source read.**

*Attributed:*
- Trump ordering his clearance in May 2018. This is the NYT's account via CNN. Kelly's memo was not released, and his lawyer's spokesman denies any pressure.
- The CIA withholding SCI access (NBC sources).

- Bilderberg 2019 attendance (AFP/AP). The official participant list refused us.
