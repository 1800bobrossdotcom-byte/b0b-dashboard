Research pass, 19 Oct 2026. **NOT PUBLISHED.** Author's brief: *"a full drill down on Israel interference going back to 1960 at least with the bombing of our ship"*.

**Method.** Every source cited here was fetched through `scripts/spider/spider.py` (note `israel-us line B`; a few re-fetches ran as `verify` because the cache in this container was cold) and read in spider text or extracted locally from the cached bytes. Search results were used only to find leads; no figure or quote below comes from a search summary. One search summary was wrong: it gave `nsarchive.gwu.edu/node/3013` as the 1964–65 Dimona briefing book, and that URL is a 2000 Polk Award page. Scanned typed letters with no text layer were read as images. Lawful advocacy by US citizens is not treated as foreign agency anywhere below. In §3 the "foreign principal" in the DOJ's own letter is the **Jewish Agency for Israel (American Section)**, not "Israel" and not American Jews.

## 1. USS LIBERTY — 8 June 1967 (priority)

### 1a. The attack — documented

**When, where, and how it unfolded:**
- **Time and position.** FRUS 1964–68 XIX doc **204** (Editorial Note) gives 8:03 a.m. EDT (1203Z). The ship was heading northwest "in international waters, a little more than 13 nautical miles from the Sinai coast, approximately 25 miles northwest of El Arish". The note cites the Navy Court of Inquiry proceedings.
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d204
- **The ship's own sitrep.** Doc **218**, *Liberty* to CNO, 1715Z:
  - "ship attacked by unidentified jet fighters, believed to be Israeli. Approximately six strafing runs";
  - three torpedo boats, "one identified as Israeli … Hull number of one boat was 206–17";
  - "One torpedo passed approx 25 yards astern approximately one minute later ship sustained torpedo hit stbd side";
  - two Israeli helicopters afterwards, "Israeli insignia clearly visible".
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d218
- **Israel's same-day report.** Doc **211**, US Defense Attaché Tel Aviv, 1414Z: "Israeli aircraft and MTB's erroneously attacked U.S. ship … Israelis send abject apologies."
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d211
- **The 100-mile orders never arrived.** Doc **217**, CINCUSNAVEUR, 8 June: JCS had ordered *Liberty* "not to proceed closer than 100 miles to Israel, Syria or UAR". The FRUS editors add: "The Liberty did not receive these messages." Doc **337**, JCS to McNamara, 1 July 1967: "Had these messages been received, the ship might have cleared the danger area prior to the attack."
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d217 · https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d337
- **The rescue sortie.** Doc **210**: the Soviet chargé was told aircraft from *Saratoga* were going "to investigate". By 11:25 a.m. the planes had been recalled, after "the Israelis had acknowledged hitting the ship in error". Doc 204 gives the 1316Z order: "Defense of USS Liberty means exactly that. Destroy or drive off any attackers."
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d210
- **NSA intercepts (aftermath only).** Doc **319**, DIRNSA, 22 June: "There are no COMINT reflections of the actual attack itself." Intercepts from 1229Z onward show:
  - the Hatzor controller calling it "a warship … which we attacked";
  - then: "the ship is now identified as Egyptian";
  - then, at 1312Z, helicopter 815 sighting "an American flag", and Hatzor asking "if this is really an American flag."
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d319

**Casualties:**
- **The dead.** FRUS editorial note to doc 424, citing the *Department of State Bulletin*, refers to "the families of the 34 men killed". The Ninth Circuit (Aug 2026, below): "Thirty-four American troops were killed and many more were injured." **34 dead is documented.**
- **The wounded: not read in any primary here.** The figures in circulation:
  - 171 (common);
  - 172 (Moorer commission and Boston affidavit, both in the *Congressional Record* 2004);
  - "approximately 174" (military.com 2026);
  - CIA's first count, 13 June: "10 killed, 90 wounded, and 22 missing" (doc 284);
  - 1969 payment: "164 claims", which counts claims, not wounded.
  The NSA history that carries the Navy figure refused the crawler (see NULLS). **Use "34 killed and more than 170 wounded" at attributed tier, or "164 injury claims paid" at documented tier.**

### 1b. US official verdicts, contemporaneous — documented

**The diplomatic notes:**
- **Rusk's note to Ambassador Harman, 10 June 1967 (original).** Doc **256**:
  > "the later military attack by Israeli aircraft on the U.S.S. Liberty is quite literally incomprehensible. As a minimum, the attack must be condemned as an act of military recklessness reflecting wanton disregard for human life."

  It demanded "the disciplinary measures which international law requires".
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d256
- **Symmetry: the US corrected its own note.** Doc **352** is the July revision, re-dated 10 June:
  > "As a minimum, the attack must be condemned as an act of military irresponsibility reflecting reckless disregard for human life."

  The FRUS note says "Most of the revisions were made to correct incorrect times and incorrect statements in the original note" and that Walt Rostow "deleted the word 'wanton'". The original's "two Israeli aircraft circled … three times … approximately one hour before the attack" became "Beginning at about 0515 hours … aircraft believed to be Israeli circled the U.S.S. Liberty on a number of occasions." Both notes were filed as "the true, corrected versions … all other copies should be destroyed". This is version control in the record; it is recorded and not read for meaning.
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d352
- **Rusk to Tel Aviv, 9 June.** Doc **239**: "very strong feeling here about the incomprehensible attack on the USS Liberty."
  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d239

**The Clifford report.** Doc **373**: Clark Clifford, Chairman of PFIAB, to Rostow, 18 July 1967, *The Israeli Attack on the USS Liberty*. Rostow forwarded it as "based on the study of literally thousands of pages of evidence"; LBJ initialled it. Findings:
- "The attack was executed with complete surprise, remarkable efficiency, devastating accuracy and deeply tragic results."
- On the A–A signal: "The Israeli claim that the Liberty transmitted an 'A–A' signal prior to the torpedo attack is demonstrably false."
- **Conclusion a:** "The information thus far available does not reflect that the Israeli high command made a premeditated attack on a ship known to be American."
- **b:** "The evidence at hand does not support the theory that the highest echelons of the Israeli Government were aware of the Liberty's true identity … To disprove such a theory would necessitate a degree of access to Israeli personnel and information which in all likelihood can never be achieved."
- **c:** "That the Liberty could have been mistaken for the Egyptian supply ship El Quesir is unbelievable."
- **d:** "The best interpretation from available facts is that there were gross and inexcusable failures in the command and control of subordinate Israeli naval and air elements."
- **f:** "The unprovoked attack on the Liberty constitutes a flagrant act of gross negligence for which the Israeli Government should be held completely responsible, and the Israeli military personnel involved should be punished."
- Findings of fact (j): "The weight of the evidence is that the Israeli attacking force originally believed their target was Egyptian."

https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d373

**CIA:**
- **13 June 1967.** Doc **284**: helicopter intercepts "leave little doubt that the Israelis failed to identify the Liberty as a US ship before or during the attack"; it "could easily be mistaken for the latter vessel by an overzealous pilot."

- **21 June 1967, revising the above.** Doc **317**:

The attack was not made in malice toward the US and was by mistake, but the failure of the IDF Headquarters and the attacking aircraft to identify the Liberty and the subsequent attack by the torpedo boats were both incongruous and indicative of gross negligence."

  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v19/d317

**Documented (FRUS 1961–63 XVIII):**

  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v18/d207

  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v18/d258

  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v18/d317

  https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v18/d391

  https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2019-05-02/battle-letters-1963-john-f-kennedy-david-ben-gurion-levi-eshkol-us-inspections-dimona

**Counter-account / symmetry:**
- Israel gave repeated written assurances that Dimona was peaceful (FRUS docs 258, 274, 289, 317).
- The US teams "found 'no direct weapons-related activities'" (EBB 733).
- The State Department chose to "give him the benefit of the doubt" (doc 317).
- That the visits were *managed* is the historians' reading of later evidence, not a finding of any US inspection.

**Grade:**
- **Documented:** US pressure and Israel's narrowing of access.
- **Attributed (expert):** systematic deception of the visiting teams.
