unreleased** ("bottled up"). "Missing" and "located" are the 2026 framing. *Withheld* and *lost* are
different claims, and "now located" moves from one to the other without a document. Naming error
in the making.

## Verdict

- **That DIA has located the AAWSAP data warehouse or the 100+ papers: not established.** Single
  source, unread at origin, from an interested party, with no agency confirmation.
- **Guaranteed null, stated in advance:** whether DIA confirms, denies or says nothing, the
  existence of a warehouse is not evidence about its contents. Locating files is not releasing
  them, and releasing them is not evidence of recovered craft. The page already says the
  collection "is the collection most often over-read".
- **What would move it:** a DIA or PURSUE statement, a FOIA response citing the records, or a
  release. The Black Vault's long FOIA line (19-F-0877; appeal granted 27 Sept 2024, remanded for a
  further search, per its own report — not read here) is the natural place for a "located"
  response to surface on paper.

**Recommendation: hold. Nothing on the page changes.** If DIA confirms, it is one line appended to
the XVI reading-room entry at its tier. If a release follows, read it before writing.
