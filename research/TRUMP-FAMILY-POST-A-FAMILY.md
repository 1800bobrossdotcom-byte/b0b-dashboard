# trumpfam A — the family record, 1885–1985

Research only. Held for the author (subject living). Every Q line below was copied from a page fetched with
`scripts/spider/spider.py` (note prefix `trumpfam A`); PDF quotes are from local text extraction of the spider-cached
file, as the spider instructs. House numbers in the 1927 sources are replaced with "[NO.]" and never printed.

**Summary.** The skeleton of the family lines holds and the adjectives mostly do not. **Holds, documented:** Friedrich
Trump was expelled from Bavaria by a royal decree of 27 Feb 1905 for not doing military service and not reporting his
1885 departure; Fred Trump was among those arrested after the Klan-police clash at the Queens Memorial Day parade of 30
May 1927 and was "discharged"; Fred testified before the Senate Banking Committee's FHA inquiry in July 1954; the
Justice Department sued Fred C. Trump, Donald Trump and Trump Management in Oct 1973 under the Fair Housing Act and the
case closed by a no-admission consent order on 10 June 1975; a deed reported by the NY Post shows Fred conveying the
Beach Haven Jewish Center's land for $10 in Oct 1955; John G. Trump examined Tesla's papers for the Alien Property
Custodian in Jan 1943. **Chronology error:** "kicked out of Bavaria … so he ran Klondike hotels" runs backwards — the
Klondike restaurants (1898–1901) came four years *before* the 1905 expulsion. **Attributed, not documented:** the
brothel reading of the Arctic rests on Gwenda Blair (biographer) and one anonymous 1900 letter to the *Yukon Sun*;
"private boxes for ladies" is reported as a period advertisement's wording, while the bed and gold scale are Blair's.
**Framing:** "women by the hour", "pay-per-minute closet", "skimming", "the Trumps had Tesla", "the Klan in the morning,
the rabbi in the afternoon". **Overstated / naming errors:** "MIT physicist" (an electrical engineer with a physics MA);
"had Tesla" (examined the papers for two days, found nothing of value, and the papers went to Tesla's nephew and then
Belgrade); "funded it for 48 years" (the 48 years is the synagogue's own phrase for the *friendship*; Fred died 1999).
**Unsupported in anything read:** Klan membership; any criminal charge from the 1954 inquiry; the "lover of Israel"
title.

## 1. "Kicked out of Bavaria for dodging the army, so he … ran Klondike Gold Rush hotels"
VERDICT: documented + chronology error — the expulsion is real and was for skipping military service and not reporting
his 1885 departure, but it came in 1905, after the Klondike years (1898–1901), not before them.
RECORD: Friedrich Trump emigrated in Oct 1885, aged 16. He ran restaurants on the White Pass trail, at Bennett (from May
1898) and Whitehorse (1900), sold out and left the Yukon in 1901, and went back to Kallstadt a rich man. He married in
1902, returned to Germany in 1904, and a royal decree dated 27 Feb 1905 (found by historian Roland Paul, printed by
Bild, reported by the Guardian) ordered him out within eight weeks; his petition to Prince Regent Luitpold failed and
the family sailed on 1 July 1905. The decree's stated ground is failure to serve plus failure to give notice of
departure; Blair (via Wikipedia) adds the formal instrument, Interior Ministry resolution 9916 of 1886.

supplemental relief filed 7 March 1978, a conference on 9 May 1978 adjourned without date, and DOJ letters on that
