## The answer in one paragraph

No document read ties Lutnick or Cantor Fitzgerald to foreknowledge of the attack, to trading on it, or to concealing
anything about it. Every circulating claim of that kind that was found at its source fails: on a misquote, on a
guaranteed null, or on the 9/11 Commission's own tracing of the trades. What the documents *do* show is a dense,
consistent primary record, with four nodes that recur in all three periods and several that only share a timeline.

## Nodes that recur on documents, before → the day → after

1. **His control of the firm.** He took control of Cantor through the 1996 Delaware action against the founder's family's
   company (settled May 1996; *Cantor Fitzgerald Inc. v. Lutnick*, 2d Cir. 2002; Iris Cantor's later suit was dismissed
   as time-barred). Through the managing general partner he signed the day's decisions (8-K, the pay stoppage, the
   pledge), and he held it until his divestiture closed on 6 Oct 2025 (13D/A).
2. **One World Trade Center.** Cantor was a tenant through the 1993 bombing (its ~$500M claim, attributed). It held
   floors 101–105, and the futures exchange's operators worked there. It lost 658 of its 960 New York-based staff,
   eSpeed's ~180 among them (his written Senate answer, eSpeed's Q3 2001 10-Q).
3. **The backup data centres.** Rochelle Park NJ and London were named in eSpeed's 10-K of **6 March 2001**, six
   months before the attack, and off-site disaster recovery was already in the CFTC's 1998 approval. They carried the
   platform back within 47 hours (8-K, Oct 2001), and the government bond market reopened on 13 Sept with eSpeed
   connected (10-Q).
4. **The US Treasury market, in four steps:**
   - inter-dealer broker in 2001 (GAO-03-251: the most disrupted of the brokers);
   - not a primary dealer until **1 Aug 2006** (NY Fed list);
   - eSpeed's Treasury platform sold to NASDAQ OMX in 2013;
   - from 2021, custodian and trading partner for "a portion" of the Treasuries backing Tether (Cantor's SEC filing).

   Lutnick framed Tether's holders as "helping finance our debt".

**Grade of the pattern.** Items 1–3 are the firm's structure. Item 4 is its business line, and a Treasury dealer
touches Treasuries in every period by definition. So the recurrence is **documented and says nothing about 11
September** (guaranteed null). It is a portrait of the firm, not a chain.
