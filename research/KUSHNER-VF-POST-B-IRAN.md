# kushner — beat B: the Iran-talks claims (graded 23 Oct 2026, HELD, research only)

**Summary.** This beat is the post's last paragraph. It does not cite the Vanity Fair excerpt, and nothing in it comes
from the excerpt. **Kushner's role in the 2026 US–Iran talks is documented.** He was present at every round from Oman on
6 Feb to the UN mediator meetings on 22 Sept, and the White House press secretary and the mediators' own ministries
announced his part. **His title is contested.** Trump said on 19 Feb "we're making Jared an envoy". Drop Site called him
"a private citizen with no official government role" on 15 July. **The "$9 billion" comes from one anonymous senior
Iranian official, speaking to Drop Site News (Scahill and Grim, 15 July 2026).** Press TV, the state broadcaster,
relayed it. The figure is Iran's estimate of all profits from alleged manipulation by "individuals close to President
Trump". **Drop Site does not say Kushner and Witkoff took it.** The attribution narrowed onto the two men with each
retelling: "primarily Kushner and Witkoff" (IBTimes UK, 17 July), then "pocketing $9 billion" (a 5 Oct Substack, the
post's own verb). **The list "oil futures, energy stocks, and prediction markets" is Drop Site's own description of
"widespread public speculation", not part of the Iranian accusation.** Separately, suspicious trading before US–Iran
announcements is documented: congressional letters to the CFTC (9 Apr, 8 and 17 Apr, 27 May) and Reuters' analysis of
exchange data (up to $7 bn of bets in March–April). **Every source read describes unidentified traders. None names
Kushner, Witkoff or anyone else.** Vance called the claim "completely bogus", the White House said no such message was
sent, and Kushner and Witkoff did not respond. **Chronology:** the post presents the accusation as breaking. It is from
15 July 2026, more than three months old.

## 1. "Kushner is deeply involved in Iran war diplomacy"
VERDICT: documented (the role); contested (the title). Kushner has sat on the US side with Witkoff in every 2026 round
read, from Oman (6 Feb) to the UNGA mediator meetings (22 Sept). Whether he holds an official post is disputed.
"Deeply" is the post's adjective, but the record bears it out.
RECORD:
- **Before the war.** Oman, 6 Feb: the US side was led by Witkoff and Kushner. Geneva, 26 Feb: Oman's foreign
  minister met them both. The war began on 28 Feb.
- **Islamabad.** Vance led the first round on 12 April, with both men present. On 24 April the press secretary
  announced that both would go back.
- **Lucerne, 21 June.** Vance headed it.
- **Doha, 30 June.** Qatar's foreign ministry said its prime minister met "US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared
  Kushner". Witkoff headed the technical track.
- **UNGA, 22 Sept.** Trump said both men met the mediators.
- **20 Oct.** A senior Iranian security source told Tasnim that Kushner is one of four US officials who might hold
  final authority.
- **Kushner's title is contested.**
  - Trump said on 19 Feb (Rev transcript of the Board of Peace meeting) "we're making Jared an envoy also envoy of

  the Reuters caption reads "Special Envoy for Peace Missions".

## 2. "Iran has accused him and Steve Witkoff of pocketing $9 billion through market manipulation"
VERDICT: attributed, overstated and not in the source as worded. The accuser is one anonymous senior Iranian official,
via Drop Site News, 15 July 2026. His $9 billion is Iran's estimate of all profits from alleged manipulation by
"individuals close to President Trump". No source says Kushner and Witkoff "pocketed" it. It is not an on-record
statement by the Iranian government. Chronology error: it is three months old, not breaking.
RECORD:
- **Who said it.** Drop Site's source is a senior Iranian official who was unnamed because he was "not authorized to
  speak publicly", and who "declined to offer specifics". The message to Vance during the late-June Lucerne talks
  accused the pair, in Drop Site's paraphrase, of being more interested in profiting than in a deal. The written
  material given to the mediators concerned "individuals close to President Trump". Drop Site could not confirm that
  the message was delivered.
- **The $9 billion is Drop Site's sentence, not a quote.** The official is quoted directly only on Iran's demand
  that "$4.5 billion of this sum" go to Iran.
- **Iranian state media.** Press TV (17 July) relayed the story "Citing an unnamed Iranian official". It rephrased the
  figure as "insider trading on the war at $9 billion". No IRNA, Tasnim, Mehr or Tehran Times item carrying the figure
  was read (see NULL).
- **The only named Iranian official on record about manipulation** is Parliament Speaker and chief negotiator
  Ghalibaf, on 23 March. He accused the US of using "fake news" to move oil markets, named no one and gave no figure.
- **How the attribution drifted.**
  - Drop Site, 15 July: "individuals close to President Trump".
  - IBTimes UK, 17 July: "primarily Kushner and Witkoff".
  - Common Dreams: "people with inside information".
  - X relays (not read): "a $9 billion insider trading operation linked to" the pair.
  - Three Dot Don Substack, 5 Oct: "pocketing $9 billion", the post's verb.
- **Scale check.** No outlet read tested the $9 billion calculation. The only independent arithmetic found is
  Reuters' (8 May): about $7 bn of oil bets in March–April "could have made hundreds of millions". The windows differ
  (Iran's runs to June), so this is a scale check, not a refutation.

## 3. "using insider knowledge of the talks to trade oil futures, energy stocks, and prediction markets"
VERDICT: the claim splits in two.
- **That someone traded:** documented as the subject of congressional letters and of reported exchange-data analysis,
  for oil futures and for prediction markets before the 28 Feb strikes. No regulator has made a finding.
- **That Kushner or Witkoff traded:** unsupported. No source read names them or anyone else.
- **Not in the source:** "oil futures, energy stocks, and prediction markets" is Drop Site's description of public
  speculation, not the Iranian accusation.
- **Overstated:** no reporting specific to energy-stock trades was found. The letters speak of oil futures and of
  equity futures and options.
RECORD:
- **Warren and Whitehouse, 9 April.** Their letter to CFTC Chair Selig covers trading before the 23 March and 7 April
  announcements. It quotes reports of "unidentified traders" and cites the NYT on six anonymous Polymarket accounts
  before the 28 Feb strikes.
- **Torres, two letters.**
  - 8 April, to the SEC and CFTC. His release dates the post 22 March; the Senate letters say 23 March.
  - 17 April, released 20 April, on $760 million traded before Iran's foreign minister announced Hormuz open. It asks
    the CFTC to "assess whether" US persons had advance knowledge. That is a question, not an identification.
- **Warnock and colleagues, 27 May.** The letter adds a 6 May trade before an Axios report and concedes the evidence
  "does not rule out" legitimate trading.
- **Reuters, 8 May.** Bets of up to $7 bn in March–April. Reuters "could not establish who placed the bets". A CFTC
  probe was reported by a source, but the CFTC has not confirmed one.
- **Selig, 16 April** (as reported by IBTimes): offenders would "face the full force of the law".
- **Bloomberg, 13 Oct, via IBTimes** (attributed): an adviser to the chairman had staff withdraw the insider-trading
  referral on the 23 March trades.
- **The CFTC's own record.** Its press-release index for 3 Aug–21 Oct 2026 shows no action on these trades. Its only
  prediction-market insider-trading order in that window (28 Aug) concerns a former White House teleprompter
  operator's Kalshi "mention market" bets on speeches. That case is unrelated to Iran.
- **Prediction markets.** The Polymarket wallets bet on the strike date, not the talks. Mitts and Ofir suggest the
  information may have come from security personnel "or … possibly foreign nationals".
- **CBS Atlanta, 27 May.** Investigators have not identified the traders.

## 4. Symmetry: responses from Kushner, Witkoff and the White House
VERDICT: documented for Vance and the White House; none found for Kushner and Witkoff. Both men "did not respond to
requests for comment" (Drop Site, 15 July), and no later statement from either was found.
RECORD:
- **Vance's denial**, on X, 16 July: "completely bogus" and the trading idea "absurd". It is quoted in Drop Site's
  update and by IBTimes. Quotes under claim 2.
- **The vice president's office**, through a US official: no message of this nature reached Vance or his team.
- **White House spokesperson Anna Kelly.** First, no such message was ever sent. In a follow-up, she said experts had
  been part of every negotiation. Drop Site printed both statements and noted that the pre-war team had no nuclear
  experts.
- **The symmetry rule, run both ways.**
