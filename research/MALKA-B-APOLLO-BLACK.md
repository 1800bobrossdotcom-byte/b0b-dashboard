# malka — B: Apollo and Leon Black

**Summary.** The board appointment is real and recent, but the name is wrong. On **20 October 2026** the board of
**Apollo Global Management, Inc.** (AGM, NYSE: APO, the holding company) appointed Meyer "Micky" Malka, founder and
managing partner of Ribbit Capital, as an **independent director, effective 1 January 2027**. The company's 8-K and its
own release both say this. As of today (26 Oct 2026) he has not yet taken the seat. There is no board called "Apollo
Global Wealth". That is the name of Apollo's business unit that builds products for individual investors (its own
releases and fund filings use it). The registered funds that unit sponsors have their own boards of trustees or
directors, and an EDGAR full-text search finds "Malka" in no Apollo-sponsored fund, BDC, REIT or Athene filing. The only
Apollo filings with his name are the Oct 2026 8-K and its exhibit. **Leon Black left the board on 21 March 2021**,
stepping down as CEO, director and chairman together, effective immediately. His board service and Malka's are about
5 years 9 months apart, so **they never sat on an Apollo board at the same time**. Black did not leave the company
entirely:
- he was one of the three "Principals" who held voting control through at least 30 Sept 2021;
- since 1 Jan 2022 he has been a party to the Stockholders Agreement;
- he is a 6.6% stockholder (as of 1 April 2026);
- he receives TRA payments and invests in Apollo funds;
- he holds an unexercised right to nominate himself or a designee to the board. In April 2026 he told the company he had
  "no current intention" of using it.

The 8-K discloses no arrangement behind Malka's selection and no Item 404(a) transactions. So "Apollo ... Leon Black" is
two facts about one company, not a relationship between two men. One fund-level co-listing turned up: Ribbit Capital IV
and four Apollo-named credit funds are among the many investors listed in Figure Technology Solutions' August 2025
investors' rights agreement. It is a document, and a weak edge. Nothing in this beat was refused.

## 4. The overlap question
- **Black's board service ended 21 March 2021. Malka's begins 1 January 2027. They have never sat on an Apollo board
  together,** and on the dates found they cannot have.
- Malka was appointed by the Board to fill a new seat. The 8-K discloses no selection arrangement. The April 2026 proxy
  records Black's statement that he had no current intention to nominate himself or a designee. **No document makes
  Malka anyone's designee.**
- **What is true and structural:** from 1 Jan 2027 Malka will be an independent director of a company in which Black is
  a 6.6% holder (as of 1 April 2026) with a contractual nomination right. That is a fact about Apollo's ownership. It is
  not a tie between the two men. "Apollo ... Leon Black" is two facts about one company.
TIER: documented (both sets of dates). A relationship between the two men: **not established**.
