## 1. VERDICT FIRST - three tests for "substantial"

**Test A. Is there a DOCUMENT - not a recollection - placing Epstein in a relationship with a
named intelligence service?**
**NOT REACHED.** The closest objects in the corpus are two, and both are the opposite of what
they look like at first glance. Epstein to Ehud Barak, 17 December 2018: *"unfortunately, not.
you should make clear that i dont work for mossad. :)"* (EFTA02612203). That is a joke and a
denial, and by the guaranteed-null rule it is non-diagnostic in both directions - a man who
worked for a service and a man who did not both write that sentence. And Epstein to Barak,
8 November 2017, on the Weinstein/Black Cube story: *"Did Boies ask you to help Obtain former
mossad agents to do dirty investigations? this is getting a lot of press"* (EFTA02571746;
vol00009-efta00964979). That is Epstein asking Barak a question, which establishes that he did
not already know the answer. **What would move this test:** a service document, a liaison cable,
a payment record, or a US government record characterising him as a source. None was found.

**Test B. Is there a DOCUMENT placing Epstein, or an actor in his circle, in 9/11 foreknowledge
or in the 9/11 record?**
**NOT REACHED, and the null is unusually clean.** See §2. The corpus holds no Epstein statement
about 9/11 at any date. The only 2001-02 documents that touch the attacks at all are two chain
forwards from members of the Maxwell family, and both are trivia.

**Test C. Is there a documented CHAIN - not a shared timeline - running Epstein to Saudi
Arabia, Israel or the CIA and from there to 9/11?**
**NOT REACHED.** Every Saudi and Israeli channel found in the corpus post-dates the attacks by
five to seventeen years. There is no 1990s or 2001 layer to chain to. **What would move this
test:** a pre-2001 document. The corpus effectively begins in the mid-2000s, so the absence is
partly an artefact of the record's own boundaries, and that limit must travel with the null.

**What WAS reached, and it is not small.** Three documented channels the report does not
currently carry, and a fourth correction to material it does carry. They are Saudi, Israeli and
Lutnick, they are all in Epstein's own office traffic, and they are in §3, §4 and §6.

## 9. METHOD, AND WHAT WAS NOT DONE

- Corpus: `POST https://jmail.world/api/emails/search`, `curl -sL` (the endpoint 307-redirects),
  exact phrase in double quotes, `limit` 50, up to four pages per phrase. Re-filtered in-script
  against `content_markdown`; deduplicated by document id and again by normalised body text. The
  API's `total` field is unreliable and is cited nowhere.
- Phrases run: 9/11 set (13), Saudi set (9), Israel set (7), CIA set (8), plus targeted follow-ups
  on Raafat, Alsabbagh, aziza, Boies, "mossad guys", "dirty investigations", "black cube",
  "ppion@cantor.com", "Pion, Paul", "OK, lunch on Sunday".
- Counts are a floor rather than a census: page limits were four per phrase and several phrases
  reported more pages than were read.
- **Not done in this pass:** the open-record half of the brief - Acosta's reported remark, the
  Austrian passport filings, the Urban Moving Systems FOIA file, the DEA art-students memorandum,
  the Alec Station withholding, the Ben-Menashe interviews, and the Whitney Webb syntheses taken
  apart claim by claim. Twelve subagents were dispatched to that work and all twelve died on a

  non-diagnostic about the underlying record. **That half remains open.**
- **Not opened:** no FD-302, no victim interview, no protective-order material. No imagery. No
