Researched 26 Oct 2026. Research only; nothing goes on the page from this pass. Jared Kushner is living and has never
been charged. His father, Charles Kushner, was convicted in 2005, pardoned in Dec 2020 and is ambassador to France;
the two are kept apart below.

**Summary.**
- **The comparison is run against the report's own criteria.** Section II
  (`ii-the-conclusion-in-one-place-two-principals-a-sponsor-question-and-a-price`) rests Epstein's rung-4 placement
  on four documented features:
  - a fabricated academic history kept after checking;
  - a fortune whose two documented clients paid on a scale nobody has explained;
  - an agreement immunising co-conspirators the government had not named;
  - "a decade of elite access after a sex-offence conviction".

  It falls on three falsifiers and rises "only on a document".
- **Kushner's record shows partial parallels to two of the four features, and each has a documented ordinary
