## THE PATTERN BEING HUNTED, AND THE THING IT IS NOT

Section II's third-form subsection names a shape and rests its whole first
proposition on it: institutions that **held derogatory information about Jeffrey
Epstein in their own files, in writing, and proceeded anyway.** Not "should have
known." Not "missed it." Not "failed to ask." The vetting *happened*, it *produced
an adverse answer*, the answer *went into the institution's own paper*, and the
institution *continued*.

Three are established in the report and were used here as the calibration set:

1. **Bear Stearns, 1976** — commissioned Fidelifacts; no record of the claimed U.C.
   Berkeley lectureship; kept him, limited partner 1981.
2. **JPMorgan, 21 May 2003** — Security Services signed off the Financial Trust
   Company Due Diligence Report with Vicky Ward's March 2003 Vanity Fair profile
   inside the approval package (Exhibit 49, *USVI v. JPMorgan*, EFTA02810791);
   banked him ~another decade.
3. **SDFL / Acosta, 2007** — held the case, wrote "This has to stop" over a drafting
   clause (EFTA00189167), signed the NPA immunising unnamed co-conspirators.

**The exclusion, stated first and applied throughout.** "They should have known" is
not the pattern and is not admitted here at any tier. Nor is "an individual inside
the institution overrode the institution's own written decision" — that is the
*inverse* shape, and it is recorded below as a counter-example rather than smuggled
in as a weak instance. Nor is post-arrest jurisdictional housekeeping, however
suggestive the verb. The test is three-part and all three parts must hold:
**(a)** the adverse fact was reduced to writing **(b)** inside the institution's own
records **(c)** before the decision that continued the relationship.

**Result: four new instances at documented tier, one at attributed, one not
established.** Two of the four are new institutions (Deutsche Bank, MIT); one is a
new organ of an institution already in the report at a different level (JPMorgan
compliance, 2010–13, distinct from the 2003 due-diligence exhibit); one is a new
kind of institution entirely (a prosecutor's office acting against its own file).

## 2. MIT — Jun 2013 to 2017 — **DOCUMENTED**

New to the report. Searched `site/report.html`: "Goodwin Procter" → 0 occurrences.
(Joi Ito appears in the report as a documented Epstein grantee; the *institutional*
decision does not.)

### First, a correction I had to make against myself

Multiple secondary outlets — and the WebSearch summary I got on the first pass —
report that MIT coded Epstein "**disqualified**" in its donor database and took his
money anyway. **That is false, and the primary report says so in terms.** Goodwin
Procter, footnote 28:

> "**Contrary to certain media reports, neither Epstein nor his foundations was ever
> coded as 'disqualified' in MIT's donor systems.** Further, the code 'disqualified'
> does not mean that a person or entity is 'blacklisted' or prohibited from donating
> to the Institute. Rather, the term 'disqualified' is a database code for any donor
> who previously donated to MIT but presently is dormant or is no longer interested
> in giving to MIT."

The claim is not used here. Recording the correction because the myth is the
attractive version and it is wrong, and because the real finding — below — is
stronger than the myth it replaces.

### What it held, and when

Source: *Report Concerning the Facts and Circumstances Surrounding Certain Donations
Made to MIT by Jeffrey E. Epstein*, Goodwin Procter LLP, 10 January 2020, 63pp.
Retrieved from `factfindingjan2020.mit.edu`. SHA-256
`41533e78…dd61f69b`. Interviews with 59 people; ~610,000 documents. The
report quotes MIT's own contemporaneous emails verbatim — which is what makes it
usable as the institution's own paper rather than a narrative about it.

"**Alert on Recent Gift**":

> "I learned today that we have accepted a recent gift from Jeffrey Epstein (a
> potentially controversial donor) of $100K for unrestricted support of Joi Ito,
> Director of the Media Lab… The PACK letter is now on hold pending your advice and
> counsel. Perhaps, you already know of this person?"

Newton to Morgan, copying Ruiz:

> "At the end of the discussion, there was a consensus, reached by Morgan, Ruiz, and

> "We will keep the money, but it will be listed as anonymous. Any further financial

> You should ask him not to publicize his support of MIT, as we are handling these

> donor's request.**"

### What it did after

Findings 1 and 5 of the report: ten donations 2002–2017 totalling $850,000, of which
**nine, totalling $750,000, came after the 2008 conviction**; and —

> "In 2013, certain members of MIT's Senior Team — Vice President and General Counsel
> R. Gregory Morgan, Vice President for Resource Development Jeffrey Newton, and
> Executive Vice President and Treasurer Israel Ruiz — were aware of, and approved,
> Epstein's donations… **These members of the Senior Team who approved MIT's
> acceptance of Epstein's donations were aware that Epstein had a criminal record
> involving sex offenses.**"

Money continued to 2017. Nine campus visits 2013–2017 (unknown to central
administration).

### Tier

**DOCUMENTED.** The institution's vetting fired exactly as designed — "Proof,
perhaps, that our system works" — produced the conviction in writing, put it before
three officers of the Senior Team, and the institution decided in writing to
continue and to make the continuation invisible in its own records. That last part is
what distinguishes this from an ordinary bad-judgment case: MIT did not merely
proceed, it altered its own donor records so that proceeding would not show.

### Bounds and fairness

Goodwin Procter's own conclusions, carried here because omitting them would be the
prosecutorial read: the three officers acted "in good faith in the absence of any
applicable MIT policies"; MIT had no controversial-donor policy at the time, so "the
decision to accept Epstein's post-conviction donations cannot be judged to be a
policy violation"; the anonymity condition was intended to *deny* Epstein
reputational benefit, not to hide MIT's conduct; and President Reif was not
contemporaneously aware. All three officers are living and uncharged, are named in a
published institutional report commissioned by their own employer, and nothing here
alleges any offence by any of them. The report's own verdict is "collective and
significant errors in judgment," and that is the ceiling this entry claims.

## 3. NEW YORK COUNTY DISTRICT ATTORNEY'S OFFICE — 18 Jan 2011 — **DOCUMENTED**

New to the report. Searched `site/report.html`: "Pickholz" → 0, "Vance" → 0.

A different species of the same shape, and worth the sweep for that reason: an
institution that held an adverse written assessment produced *for* it by another
state body, and went into open court to argue against it.

### What it held

`EFTA00095656` (DataSet 9, 38pp) — the New York State Division of Criminal Justice
Services' certified production of Epstein's Sex Offender Registry file, sworn 8
January 2019 by a DCJS Office Assistant under CPLR 4518(c). At Bates page
`EFTA00095662` it contains the **New York State Board of Examiners of Sex Offenders'
transmittal to New York County Supreme Court, dated 19 August 2010**:

> "TO: NEW YORK COUNTY SUPREME COURT   DATE: 8/19/2010 … RE: JEFFREY E EPSTEIN …
> SUPERVISING AGENCY: NONE, OFFENDER IS UNSUPERVISED.
> The above referenced individual has committed an offense in another jurisdiction
> and has established residence in New York State… **The NYS Board of Examiners of
> Sex Offenders is recommending a risk level and designation for the offender, as
> indicated on the attached risk assessment instrument, case summary and designation
> form.**"

The Board's recommendation was Level 3 — the highest risk tier. It went to the court,
and therefore to the parties, five months before the hearing.

The same file also carries the defence's own written framing of the Florida
predicate, in a 28 July 2010 letter from Martin G. Weinberg to DCJS
(`EFTA00095668`), setting out the two Florida counts and which one triggered
registration — so the office also had the conviction record itself, in writing,
before it.

### What it did after

On 18 January 2011, at the SORA hearing in New York County Supreme Court, the
District Attorney's office asked the court to adjudicate Epstein a **Level 1** sex
offender — the lowest tier — against the Board's Level 3 recommendation. Justice Ruth
Pickholz, from the bench:

> "I have to tell you; I am a little overwhelmed because **I have never seen the
> prosecutor's office do anything like this. I have never seen it.**"

She denied the application and adjudicated him Level 3. Affirmed unanimously,
*People v Epstein*, 2011 NY Slip Op 08293 (1st Dept, 17 Nov 2011).

In an August 2011 appellate brief the office conceded in writing that in reaching its
position it had "relied on a combination of a mistaken interpretation of the
governing legal standards and certain secondhand information about the Florida
case."

### Tier

**DOCUMENTED** as to the holding (the Board's written recommendation is a primary in
the DOJ production, hashed) and as to the outcome (a published appellate decision and
a hearing transcript quoted consistently across CNN, Law & Crime and the First
Department record). **Attributed** as to the exact wording of the transcript, which I
did not obtain in original form — `nycourts.gov` and `law.justia.com` both returned
HTTP 403 to the fetcher, and `cnn.com` returned HTTP 451. The quotation above is
reported identically by multiple outlets and appears in an appellate filing, which is
why it is carried; it should be replaced with the transcript before publication.

### Note on shape

This is the pattern in its advocacy form rather than its relationship form: the
institution did not "continue a relationship," it acted against the contents of its
own file. I judge that inside the pattern — the report's proposition is about
*institutions declining to act on what they already held*, and asking a court to
downgrade the man your file assesses as highest-risk is the sharpest available
version of that. But the difference is real and is flagged rather than blurred.

### Living-person floor

The Assistant District Attorney who made the application is named in the public
record and in contemporaneous reporting. She is living and uncharged and is **not
named here**. The finding is about the office, the office is what the report is
documenting, and naming the individual line prosecutor buys the argument nothing.

Distinct from calibration item 2. The 2003 item is a due-diligence file at

**DOCUMENTED**, but at *court-filing* tier rather than *primary-document* tier. The

## 5. BEAR STEARNS — March 1981 — **ATTRIBUTED**

The prompt flagged the 1981 suspension file. It is a second, distinct episode at the
institution that supplies calibration item 1, and it has the right shape — but I
could not get it to primary.

**What is reported:** an executive-committee review concluded that a personal loan
Epstein made to a friend breached Regulation D and that prior expense-account
"carelessness" warranted discipline; the firm imposed a **60-day suspension and a
$2,500 fine** in March 1981. Epstein resigned as a limited partner on 12 March 1981,
reportedly a day after the SEC opened an inquiry into trading around the Seagram
tender offer for St. Joe Minerals. Bear Stearns then **kept him as a client**, with
the firm's traders executing block orders for his new money-management vehicle.

**Why it is only attributed.** No firm document has surfaced. The disciplinary
finding, the fine and the suspension come from Vicky Ward's reporting and the 2019
wave of secondary accounts derived from it; the "kept him as a client" limb rests on
later journalism and on one personal research site. Bear Stearns no longer exists,
its records went to JPMorgan in 2008, and nothing in the DOJ production I searched
carries the 1981 material.

**Tier: ATTRIBUTED.** Do not upgrade without a firm record or a contemporaneous
regulatory filing.

## 6. GOVERNMENT OF THE U.S. VIRGIN ISLANDS — 2012 to 2018 — **NOT ESTABLISHED**

**The shape is right and the documents are missing, which is why it is here rather
than in the findings.**

Epstein was a sex offender registered *in the USVI* — the territory's own Department
of Justice held his registration file, and the corpus shows his counsel filing travel
notices into it (`EFTA02467947`, 20 Apr 2018; `EFTA02514233`, 14 Jan 2015;

Epstein's USVI office manager, "Jean[n]e and I will be out of the office… for the VI
Economic Development Commission's annual VIEDC Beneficiary Summit."

**Why it fails the test.** I have the registration in one territorial agency's file

EDC's own application or approval file**. Two agencies of one government is not the
same as one institution's file, and the pattern requires the latter. The USVI
Attorney General's January 2020 civil action pleads that Epstein made
misrepresentations to the territory — which points the other way, toward the
territory as deceived rather than as knowing. JPMorgan's June 2023 third-party
pleading alleges USVI officials enabled him, but that is an adverse party's
allegation in litigation it was defending, and it is not evidence here.

**Tier: NOT ESTABLISHED.** What would settle it: the EDC application and approval
file for Southern Trust Company (2012) and the renewal file (through 2018). Open
lead, worth chasing.

# TESTED AND EXCLUDED — with the reason in each case

These are not nulls. Each is a line that returned material, and the material failed
the three-part test. Recording them because the exclusions are the part of an

**Harvard University — excluded, and it is a counter-example.** Harvard's May 2020
*Report Concerning Jeffrey E. Epstein's Connections to Harvard University* (Diane E.
Lopez, VP & General Counsel) records that President Drew Faust, briefed on the
allegations between July 2007 and November 2008, **decided Harvard would no longer

Evolutionary Dynamics, more than 40 visits 2010–2018 including six in 2018 — is the

**Palm Beach County Sheriff's Office, work release 2008–09 — excluded.** The

in 2019 — but it is not this pattern. Textbook "should have known / should have done
better." Excluded.

matters.** `EFTA00018169` (DataSet 8, 1p) is genuine and I have it hashed. It reads:

through the press. Consolidating investigative control of a live federal prosecution
is ordinary and is arguably required. **It is not an institution holding derogatory
information and proceeding anyway, and it is not admitted at any tier.** Flagged
here because it is the single most likely item in this space to be mis-cited into
the pattern.

system. They are recorded here as an index into the relationship, not as evidence of
one. The evidence is the RRC documents in §1.

# STANDING-RULE COMPLIANCE

- **Living-person floor held throughout.** No living, uncharged person is asserted
  here to be a criminal or an intelligence asset. Every named individual appears as
  the author or recipient of their own institutional correspondence, or as an officer
  named in a published report commissioned by their own institution. The line
  prosecutor in §3 is deliberately not named. The Investment Bank CEO in §4 appears
  only inside a quotation of someone else's characterisation. Findings are stated
  against institutions and processes, which is what the pattern is about.
- **No hex32 ids cited.** Every citation in this document is EFTA or a published
  court/report reference. hex32 ids were not used as sources at any point.
- **No imagery mined.** `EFTA01420192` is 31 pages with 31 embedded images; checked
  — each is the ~70KB page-scan raster of a printed email, and text extraction
  returned 30,237 characters of prose. It is a document, not an image set. No
  document in this sweep was substantially imagery, so the stop-and-delete rule was
  not triggered.
- **No 302s, no victim interviews, no protective-order material read.**
  `EFTA00095656` was opened, checked for protective-order stamps and 302 structure
  (none present — it is a DCJS records-custodian certification of registry
  paperwork), and only registry-administrative pages were read. **The 1996 FBI
  referral line was deliberately left unopened** because its obvious route runs
  through survivor testimony. Maria Farmer's 2025 correspondence surfaced in a
  corpus search; it was not opened beyond the search preview and is not used — she is
  a survivor and her personal correspondence is not evidence about an institution's
  files.
- **No DOJ redaction de-anonymised.** Several documents carry redacted names
  (`EFTA01345162`, `EFTA01399500`); none was inferred or reconstructed.
- **Nulls reported as findings**, including the technical ones and including the
  guaranteed-null caveat on the single-bank result.
- **One myth affirmatively refuted against myself** (MIT "disqualified"), because the
  first-pass search returned the attractive false version and the primary contradicts
  it.
