## 5. Primary dealer (2006) and Newmark (2011–2018)

The New York Fed added Cantor Fitzgerald & Co. to its primary-dealer list effective 1 August 2006, and it is on the
current list. Primary dealers are the Fed's monetary-policy counterparties and are expected to bid pro rata in every
Treasury auction. Separately, BGC bought Newmark Knight Frank (closed 14 Oct 2011) and spun Newmark Group off to its
shareholders on 30 Nov 2018; Newmark is commercial real estate, not the Treasury market.
