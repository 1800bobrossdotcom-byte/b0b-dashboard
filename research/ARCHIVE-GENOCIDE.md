## The method — and why it matters to this report

It runs, independently, several of the report's own rules:

| Archive Genocide | This report |
|---|---|
| Clips merged only on **recomputable signals** (perceptual fingerprint 3/5 frames within Hamming 10; same post URL; same date + same resolved place) — "merging two distinct massacres into one node asserts something that did not happen" | the naming error / anti-map: a shared label or timeline is not an identity |
| **Links graded**: *established* (deterministic) vs *possible* (dashed, "a lead worth checking, never a finding") | documented / labeled tiering; hex32-is-a-lead rule |
| **Corroboration tiers A–E**, where A = 2+ independently filmed clips from 2+ independent archives; a daily report for the date "says the day is documented, not that this event is" | the symmetry rule applied to its own evidence |
| **"Figures this archive refused to publish"** — casualty numbers found, examined and rejected with reasons, "because an archive that only ever shows what it accepted is asking to be trusted" | §8: authority from what it refuses to allege; published nulls |
| No model-generated coordinates; places resolve to OpenStreetMap ids | provenance as the asset |
| People and perpetrators "kept in separate classes and never merged"; a person node needs ≥3 name tokens | — |

**That last table row is the keepable finding:** an adversarial-to-power documentation project,
on the opposite side of a war from most institutions this report examines, independently built
the same discipline — and published its rejected figures. It is a method specimen of the kind
Section I describes, found in the wild.

## Limits and hard rules if any of it is used

1. **Tier: attributed.** Every count and every incident on the map is the archive's own claim.
   A *grade A* event is strong corroboration of *footage*; it is not a finding about who did it.
2. **The 715 "actors" / "named perpetrators" class does not enter this report at any tier.**
   Most are living and uncharged; a graph edge — even a solid one — is "named as acting" in
   text, not a finding. Living-person floor, and the map's own "possible" links are leads by its
   own definition. **No individual soldier's or officer's name from this archive goes on the page.**
3. **"Genocide" is carried as the archive's word, and as the legal question actually pending**
   (South Africa v. Israel at the ICJ; provisional measures orders of 26 Jan, 28 Mar and 24 May 2024
   find the rights claimed *plausible* — that is not a merits finding). Neither adopted nor
   refused: tier-stated. *Status of the merits phase in 2026 not verified here — check before use.*
4. **Do not link the torrents or the raw DDoSecrets volumes from the page.** Link the method
   pages (`/nodes/`, `/verify.html`); a reader who wants the footage can find it from there.
