## 0. Posture in four lines

1. **What the record documents about him** is a modelling agent turned real-estate broker, UN-mission minister-counsellor (Dominica, 2011), Kennedy Center trustee (named 22 Dec 2020) and, from March 2025 to 15 Oct 2026, a presidentially designated envoy at State. His contacts with Epstein are **documented, small and mutually hostile in tone**: a 2004 bid contest over Elite, two Epstein emails of Feb 2011 warning an associate off him, and a 2010 forward at his request. Nothing read shows a payment, a flight, a meeting or a message *from* Epstein *to* him.
2. **The report's charge rests on FBI interview summaries (FD-302s) that this note did not open** (stop rule b) and on one witness's account to minority staff. It is a **minority-party staff report**, not a finding of the Committee, and its own verb is "may have been".
3. **He is separately a defendant in an unadjudicated civil complaint** (S.D.N.Y., filed 3 Sept 2026) and the subject of a reported ICE-contact story. He denies both. Neither is Epstein-related except by the complaint's own framing.
4. **Two of the report's own details are wrong on the record** (the envoy's title in one passage and the filing date of the civil complaint), and the claim that he introduced Melania Knauss to Trump is **contested, not settled either way** (section 3).

## 2. Government roles

**Kennedy Center (Trump I).** The White House's 22 Dec 2020 release lists "Paolo Zampolli, of New York, to be a Member of the Board of Trustees of the John F. Kennedy Center". Documented. Wikipedia adds the President's Council on Sports, Fitness & Nutrition (Jan 2021); the complaint repeats it; the White House release for that was not found (Wikipedia cites a dead health.gov link). HJC says this appointment came "at Melania's urging"; **no source is given for that clause in the report** (unsupported as written).

**UN, Dominica.** Documented: Minister Counsellor in the Permanent Mission of the Commonwealth of Dominica, Blue Book No. 301, March 2011. Wikipedia places an appointment as UN Ambassador (and "Ambassador for Oceans and Seas") in Oct 2013; no primary read. The "ambassador" title used earlier (2010–11) is an NGO honorific per Prospect.

**Trump II, "envoy".** Trump's Truth Social announcement (March 2025, not fetched; quoted by CNN): "With his extensive experience working with the United Nations, the Kennedy Center, and various International initiatives, Paolo will help us advance America's Interests on the Global stage." Wikipedia dates the start to 11 Mar 2025.
- **The title is not settled in the sources.** Trump, CNN and the embassy releases say "Special Envoy for Global Partnerships". Wikipedia's infobox and the Politico summary say "Special Representative for Global Partnerships", the predecessor post held by a Biden-era appointee. The State Dept's own listing (a congressional report) and the embassy pages refused the spider (403). **The formal instrument (appointment paper, Federal Register notice, State organisation entry) was not found.** He is not a Senate-confirmed officer.
- **HJC calls the post "Global Envoy for Strategic Partnerships" on p.14 and "Special Envoy for Global Partnerships" on p.12.** The first is a misnaming.
- **Feb 2025.** He styled himself "Special Envoy to Italy" before the appointment, and Italian press repeated it; the designation was not official (Wikipedia, citing Il Post). AGI, 12 Mar 2025: Trump named him "special envoy for global partnerships" a day after he had been presented as envoy to Italy.
- **What he did:** Uzbekistan trips (mineral and aircraft deals), advocacy for Russia's return to sports events, proposing Italy replace Iran at the World Cup, Vance's Hungary trip (7 Apr 2026), Davos. The Prospect called it "an undefined State Department role". Landau's emails (via the Daily Beast) told him to confine himself to cultural diplomacy.
- **Why he left ("until recently").** On 15 Oct 2026 Politico published a year of Landau–Zampolli emails; hours later "a US official" said his status as envoy "has been terminated". His account: "Special Envoys appointments are for one year. President Trump extended it for more than six months." The feud: Landau accused him of acting outside his remit and of "potentially unlawful business and economic activities without authorization" (Daily Beast's paraphrase of a November email); he accused Landau of leaking to the NYT and asked the State IG to investigate him; Landau called that "bizarre" and "defamatory" and denied it. An administration official called the episode "cartoonish and embarrassing". **No official reason is on record; the termination followed the Politico story by hours and preceded the Judiciary report by 11 days.** A shared timeline is not a chain: the sources do not tie the termination to the Epstein material or to the civil complaint.

## 3. The Melania introduction: two accounts, both attributed

- **His account (since 2016):** a Sept 1998 Fashion Week party he hosted at the Kit Kat Club. "Melania meet Donald, Donald meet Melania" (Daily Beast, Apr 2026); "Melania, this is Donald. Donald, this is Melania" (Folha, Jan 2017). He offered in April 2026 to testify to Congress.
- **Melania Trump's statement (9 Apr 2026, as quoted by the Daily Beast):** "I met my husband, by chance, at a New York City party in 1998." It names no introducer; the Daily Beast notes her memoir "recounts meeting Trump at a New York party that year, but not that it was Zampolli who introduced them" and that the details "point to Zampolli as the organizer of the party". Her statement agrees with his on year and kind of event and is **silent on who introduced them**.
- **HJC / the FD-302 account:** "a former longtime Epstein assistant told the FBI that Epstein introduced Melania to Donald Trump, contradicting Zampolli's account." The report adds that his former partner of 19 years said the same ("It was Jeffrey Epstein"), and calls the two "two women who had a sexual relationship with Zampolli". **Weights:**
  - the 302 is an agent's *summary* of an unsworn interview (false statements to federal agents are separately criminal, 18 U.S.C. §1001; mine, not read from a source here), not a transcript. The Daily Beast's "effectively under penalty of perjury" is its own gloss.
  - the second source is an interested party in a custody dispute with him and is, in the same recordings, making further claims about the First Lady that are **not carried here** (unsupported, a living person, and the report does not rely on them).
  - his own account is also interested.
- **Tier: contested.** The 302 text is unread; the best settler would be Knauss's own first-hand account, a 1998 guest list or photograph, or the unredacted 302. **Even if Epstein did the introducing, it would not make either man a co-conspirator in anything.** That inference is the report's, not the evidence's.

## 6. SPECULATIVE LEADS — leads, not findings

Each lead is a hypothesis a reader will chase. **None is asserted.** No living person is asserted to have committed a crime. Tier = where the hypothesis stands now.

**L1 — The modelling-agency and visa pipeline as the mechanism.**
- *Hypothesis:* agencies that sponsor visas and house young foreign models create dependence that a predator can exploit; Zampolli's agency was one such channel to Epstein.
- *Documented pieces:* Zampolli sponsored one H-1B (his own claim); his agency housed models in shared apartments (Prospect, HJC — described from FBI records, not opened); Trump is quoted by one witness saying "Paolo will take care of that" (visa). Mother Jones (2016) documents the same structural dependence at **Trump's own agency** (models on tourist visas who say it knew); that is **not** Zampolli.
- *Tier now:* **unsupported** as a trafficking mechanism for any named person; the structural dependence is documented industry-wide. **Symmetry:** a mechanism common to the trade singles out no individual.
- *PROVE:* a visa petition, agency contract or lease showing a specific model placed with Epstein through ID Models; Epstein-side payments to ID or Zampolli entities; sworn testimony that he directed a named model to Epstein knowing a sexual purpose.
- *KILL:* the unredacted 302 passages showing the women reached Epstein independently of him; ID Models' standard contracts and visa files; bank records showing no payments.

**L2 — Diplomatic posts and immunity.**
- *Hypothesis:* his UN-mission rank (2011 onward) or his envoy status gave him protection from questioning or process.
- *Tier now:* **unsupported.** Documented: Minister Counsellor (2011). The texts that would govern (the UN Headquarters Agreement and the Vienna Convention's article on nationals and permanent residents) were **not read here**; named only as what to read, not as a finding. His citizenship and its date were not established (a fact-check site refused).
- *PROVE:* a document in which DOJ/State/NYPD records an immunity claim or a declined process attributable to a post.
- *KILL:* naturalisation before 2011 and no record of any request for process.

**L3 — The 1990s Elite/Casablancas network and the 2004 bid.**
- *Hypothesis:* the Elite NY auction was an attempt to take over a pipeline of young models; Epstein and Zampolli were allies in it.
- *Tier now:* the bid contest is **attributed** (2004 press); alliance versus rivalry is **contested** (§4).
- *PROVE:* the Chapter 11 docket for Elite Model Management (S.D.N.Y., 2004) with the bid papers naming Epstein and Zampolli as one bidder; Epstein's or his entities' funding commitment.
- *KILL:* separate bids on the docket; a bid that excludes Epstein.
- *Also:* the Elite lawsuits of 2001–04 (the model suits MailOnline cites) for any deposition naming Zampolli. Not read.

**L4 — Financial ties.**
- *Hypothesis:* money moved between Epstein or Maxwell and Zampolli's entities (the bid; TerraMar; his real-estate firm, which used models as brokers).
- *Tier now:* **unsupported** for money. TerraMar partnership **attributed**; the 2012 Maxwell emails **unauthenticated**.
- *PROVE:* TerraMar corporate and tax filings listing him; authenticated DOJ-library or estate emails between Maxwell and him; Epstein estate bank records showing any transfer.
- *KILL:* filings showing no role; authenticated emails limited to promotional outreach; bank records with no counterparty.

**L5 — "Pageant" frame (Trump-hosted contests, FashionTV judging).**
- *Hypothesis:* pageants were recruiting venues; Zampolli judged and fixed visas.
- *Tier now:* the 1993 account is **testimony** (one witness; Time unable to verify); the FashionTV judging is **attributed** (Prospect).
- *PROVE:* a contemporaneous document (programme, travel or visa paper) placing a named contestant in Epstein's circle through a pageant; corroborating witnesses at the event.
- *KILL:* programme/guest records showing the persons at issue were elsewhere; an account of independent contact.

**L6 — The envoy role: conflicts and unlawful-business accusations.**
- *Hypothesis:* the Landau feud was about real conflicts (energy, nuclear and investment dealings) rather than turf.
- *Tier now:* **attributed** — Landau's written accusation is documented as an accusation; Zampolli's rejection is his.
- *PROVE:* Politico's emails in full; any State IG finding; contracts or fee agreements tied to his deals.
- *KILL:* an IG closing or State statement that the activities were authorised.

**L7 — Who introduced the First Lady (§3).**
- *Tier now:* **contested.** *PROVE (Epstein):* unredacted 302 plus a contemporaneous Epstein-side record; *KILL:* Knauss's first-hand account, a 1998 guest list or photographs, a retraction by the assistant. Either outcome is silent on conspiracy.

**L8 — The timing of the firing.**
- *Hypothesis:* the termination was a response to the Epstein material or the lawsuit.
- *Tier now:* **unsupported.** The documented trigger is the Politico story. *PROVE:* a termination memo or an official statement giving the reason; *KILL:* the same, naming the Landau dispute. **Anti-map:** adjacent dates are not a chain.
