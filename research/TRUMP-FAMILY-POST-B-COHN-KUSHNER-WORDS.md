# trumpfam — beat B: Roy Cohn, Charles Kushner, Trump's own quoted words, the religious claims (1983–2019)

Research only, held for the author. Every quote below was copied from a page fetched with the spider (ledger notes
prefixed `trumpfam B:`). Nothing here goes on the page.

**Summary.** Most of this beat holds at documented tier, and the errors are of size and naming, not invention.
**Holds (documented):** Cohn as McCarthy's chief counsel (1953–54), his Mafia clientele, his June 1986 disbarment and
its four grounds, and his role as the Trumps' counsel in the 1973 DOJ housing suit; his death on 2 August 1986 of
AIDS-related causes while publicly attributing his illness to liver cancer; Charles Kushner's 2004 guilty plea, the
witness-retaliation scheme as he admitted it, the 2005 sentence, the 23 Dec 2020 pardon and the 19 May 2025
confirmation as ambassador to France; Trump's 2006 line about Ivanka on *The View* (near-verbatim; his spokesman
said he was joking); the 2002 *New York* line on Epstein (the post drops "it is even said"; Trump said in 2019 he had
"a falling out" with him); the 2018 Mikdash "Temple Coin" pairing Trump with Cyrus; "I am the chosen one" on 21 Aug
2019 (the official transcript records "[ Laughter ]" and he later tweeted he was "kidding, being sarcastic"); three
marriages. **Naming errors / overstated:** the 2005 Stern remark was about the adult pageants (Miss USA, Miss
Universe), not a teen pageant — the separate 1997 Miss Teen USA account is **attributed and contested**; "six
bankruptcies" is six Chapter 11 filings by Trump companies, no personal bankruptcy; "God's chosen vessel" is a
critic's phrase for evangelical leaders, not their own words, though the Cyrus/"chosen" framing is theirs; the 1983
JNF award was a regional honour reported as following donations to JNF projects, not "bankrolling Israel".
**Attributed / contested:** the Plaza Hotel parties rest on one witness (Susan Rosenstiel, in Anthony Summers's 1993
Hoover biography), contested by the Justice Department official who had her claims checked and by others, and her
account casts Cohn as host and introducer, not as the "ringmaster" of a blackmail operation; "never admit, never
apologize, always attack" is not a quotation of Cohn or of Trump, but a compression of later summaries by Cohn's
associates and biographers. **Not verified from here (null):** the line "a very big award in terms of everything that
I stand for" and "still hangs in his office" (the outlet carrying it refused the spider; the video was not reached).

## 1. "Roy Cohn. McCarthy's attack dog, mob lawyer"
VERDICT: documented [+ framing for "attack dog"] — Cohn was chief counsel to McCarthy's Senate investigations
subcommittee in the 1953–54 hearings, defended reputed Mafia bosses, was disbarred on 23 June 1986, and was
defence counsel to Fred and Donald Trump in the 1973 Justice Department housing suit.
RECORD: The Senate Historical Office describes McCarthy and "his chief counsel Roy Cohn" running the subcommittee's
1953–54 hearings; UPI's 1986 obituary has him resigning in 1954. UPI names Carmine Galante and Anthony Salerno among
his clients (both dead). A unanimous five-judge panel of the Appellate Division disbarred him on Monday 23 June 1986
on four grounds: a 1975 codicil to Lewis Rosenstiel's will obtained in hospital, a $100,000 client loan unrepaid for
18 years, misstatements on a D.C. bar application, and violation of a court escrow order. *United States v. Fred C.
Trump, Donald Trump, and Trump Management* (E.D.N.Y. 1:73-cv-01529) was filed 15 Oct 1973; the Clearinghouse
docket lists Cohn as attorney for the defendants; the $100 million counterclaim was dismissed and a consent decree was
signed 10 June 1975. "Attack dog" is characterisation and is not graded.

## 2. "alleged ringmaster of sex-blackmail parties at the Plaza Hotel"
VERDICT: attributed + contested [+ overstated] — the Plaza story is one witness's account: Susan Rosenstiel, quoted in
Anthony Summers's *Official and Confidential* (1993), says she attended Plaza Hotel parties in 1958 and a year later
at which Cohn was present and J. Edgar Hoover cross-dressed; Summers framed the episodes as material the Mafia held
over Hoover, not as Cohn running a blackmail operation.
RECORD: Ronald Kessler (HNN, 23 July 2002) records that Rosenstiel served time for perjury in a 1971 case, that
Summers paid her for interviews and to hold the story, that the Justice Department official who had her claims
examined (William Hundley) called it "made up", that Robert Morgenthau found her claims baseless, and that Summers
told him he holds "no firm view" on whether she told the truth. *The Smart Set* (2010) calls the story entirely
single-witness. The stronger "ring" framing — Cohn running a sex-blackmail operation from a "blue suite" at the
Plaza, including minors — appears in Whitney Webb's *One Nation Under Blackmail*, relaying (a) former NYPD
detective James Rothstein's account to John DeCamp of what Cohn allegedly told him, and (b) lawyer John Klotz's
account to Burton Hersh. These were read here **only as a reader's transcription of Webb on Goodreads**; Webb, Hersh
(2007) and DeCamp were not read. Each is second-hand at best. The Cohn Wikipedia article carries a separate claim
(Bill Bonanno said Cohn told him of compromising photographs of Hoover) but no Plaza claim. Grade for the post's
line: Rosenstiel's claim attributed and contested; "ringmaster of sex-blackmail parties" overstated against her
account; the ring allegations attributed, unread at source.
