# NARA Record Group 615 (UAP Records Collection): bulk downloads pulled 23 Oct 2026

**Pulled at the author's instruction** (*"Pull from these places - they are public"*), by a one-off identified `curl` (UA `b0b.dev-research (user-directed download; https://www.b0b.dev)`), **not the spider**. `catalog.archives.gov/robots.txt` is `Disallow: /`, so the spider refuses it. That is an access control and was not worked around. **So this is NOT the 2026 PURSUE tranches.** The NARA bulk files were last updated 24 Apr 2025 (per NARA's page), and their coverage ends Jan 2025 at the latest.

Files are stored outside the repo at `/home/user/.b0b-sources/uap/nara/`. Project Blue Book stills (542184.zip, 36.48 GB) were not pulled.
