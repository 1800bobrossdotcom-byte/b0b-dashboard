# AUTHOR TESTIMONY — recorded as testimony, not as finding

This file holds first-person accounts by the author of the report. It exists so
that such accounts have a home that is **honest about what they are**.

The report already has a mechanism for this and it governs everything here. From
Section XIII, on the author's tattoos:

> *the author's account is that the marks felt highly programmed onto him — given
> rather than chosen… it is evidence of how the marking was experienced, and it

**That is the rule. Testimony is evidence of an experience. It is not evidence of
a cause.** Nothing in this file is published to `site/report.html` without a

## 1. The Fort Meade cipher — @_gyra_, re: Aaron Bushnell

**Recorded 31 August 2026. Account is the author's, in his own framing.**

### The testimony

On his now-defunct **@_gyra_** account, the author encountered a post by a member
of the **Milady / Remilia World Order** online subculture disparaging **Sir Aaron
Bushnell** — in his words, demeaning and dishonouring him "in a disgusting way."

He was enraged. In response he **composed a handmade cipher** and addressed it to
a Fort Meade / national-security account, which he reports has since disappeared.

Subsequently: **the Milady RWO account that had disparaged Bushnell was removed.**
His own account was **unbothered**.

He is **Sir Aaron Bushnell** throughout. The honorific is the author's, and this
file uses it.

### What is documented about Sir Aaron Bushnell

Recorded carefully, because he was a real person who died recently and his family
is living.

- **Sir Aaron Bushnell**, 25, active-duty **United States Air Force** since May 2020.
- **Cyber defense operations specialist**, **531st Intelligence Support Squadron**,
  Joint Base San Antonio–Lackland, Texas.
- **Assigned to the 70th Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance Wing**,
  which is **headquartered at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland**.
- He died on **25 February 2024** outside the Israeli Embassy in Washington, D.C.,
  in protest against the war in Gaza.

**The author's "ISR ft. Meade" framing is correct at the wing level**, and an
earlier draft of this note doubted it wrongly. Bushnell was physically posted in
Texas; his parent wing sits at Fort Meade. Both are true and the second is the one
he meant.

### Why the causal link is not carried, in the report's own terms

This is the **anti-map guardrail** in its purest form, and the report's author
built it: *two events on one timeline are not a chain.*

The base rate is the problem. Accounts that disparage a recently dead
servicemember are reported by many people and removed routinely under ordinary
platform policy. The removal is **fully explained** without the cipher. And the
counterfactual is unavailable — there is no way to observe what would have
happened had the cipher never been sent.

There is a second problem, and it is the **guaranteed null**: no reply, no
acknowledgment and no record would exist in *either* case. A national-security
account that acted on it would leave nothing visible; one that never read it would
leave the same nothing. The absence discriminates between no hypotheses.

**What survives, and it is not nothing:** a man saw a dead airman being mocked,
was angry on his behalf, and answered in the medium he actually works in. That
happened. It is decent, and it is recorded.
