## 1. Underground is not new — documented, and the oldest cases cut against the frame

**Derinkuyu** (Wikipedia, fetched):
- It may first have been cut by Phrygians in the 8th–7th century BC, and was fully formed in the Byzantine era.
- It is about 85 m deep, with room for about 20,000 people and their livestock.
- It was enlarged c. 780–1180 to shelter Christians from Umayyad and Abbasid raiders, and used again against the Seljuks and Timur.
- Cappadocian Greeks and Armenians were still using it in the 20th century. Dawkins recorded a 1909 flight underground after the Adana massacres.
- It was rediscovered in 1963, when a resident broke through a basement wall.

**Other sites on the page:**
- **Tura** (X): Old Kingdom galleries, mapped in WWII as bomb-proof storage.
- **Cold War continuity bunkers** (IX): Raven Rock, Mount Weather, Cheyenne Mountain.

**Finding.** Going under rock to outlast a threat is documented for at least ~2,700 years. **But the
oldest documented underground cities sheltered the hunted, not the rulers.** The state bunker built
for the state's own continuity is the 20th-century inversion of that use. The report's own Tura rule
holds: continuity of use is not continuity of purpose.

## 2. How far back continuity of power goes

**Code of Hammurabi §117** (c. 1754 BC; King translation, Yale Avalon, fetched): *"If any one fail to
meet a claim for debt, and sell himself, his wife, his son, and daughter for money or give them away
to forced labor: they shall work for three years in the house of the man who bought them, or the
proprietor, and in the fourth year they shall be set free."*
- The oldest well-known law code already treats human labour as collateral for debt, and caps it.
- *Not re-read today:* that the earliest Mesopotamian writing is mostly administrative accounting is
  the standard view. It is carried here as background, not as a citation.

**ILO / Walk Free / IOM, 12 Sept 2022** (fetched):
- 50 million people were in modern slavery in 2021: 28 million in forced labour and 22 million in forced marriage.
- 86% of forced labour is in the private sector; 14% is state-imposed.
- 3.3 million of those in forced labour are children.

**Finding.** The documented continuity is **the instrument, not a lineage**: debt, the labour levy and the
ledger, unbroken in form for ~3,800 years of paper. This is the report's own VII apex clause (the top is
rules, not a person), and it needs no hidden owner.

## 4. "Treating humans as slave labour": the idea has a traceable genealogy

1. **Atra-Hasis** (18th century BC, Akkadian; Wikipedia, fetched). The working gods revolt, and a first
   human couple is made "to serve as their labour force". **Documented as a text.** It is myth: what
   is documented is that the story exists, not its events.
2. **Sitchin, *The 12th Planet* (1976)** (Wikipedia, fetched). He reads the Anunnaki as extraterrestrials
   from "Nibiru".
   - The Babylonians associated Marduk with Jupiter.
   - Assyriologists and Sumerologists reject his translations as pseudohistory.
   - **Naming error:** the gods of a story read as visitors.
3. **Icke** (Wikipedia, fetched). His Anunnaki/Archon reptilians run a hybrid "Babylonian Brotherhood"
   elite.
   - *The Robots' Rebellion* (1994) endorsed the Protocols of the Elders of Zion, and his publisher then
     declined further books.
   - Per Barkun, he took both the extraterrestrial angle and the Protocols from Cooper's *Behold a Pale
     Horse* (1991), whose chapter 15 reproduces the Protocols in full.
   - He identified the Rothschilds as reptilians. Icke denies antisemitism.

**Finding.** "Non-human masters using humans as labour" runs **myth → mistranslation → the forgery the
report already killed.** It is the "families" trope with scales, and the report refuses it for the same
