## WHAT THE DOCUMENTED RECORD SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**Supports:**
- **Trump and Bayrock/Sater, 2000s–2011:** a decade of business run out of Trump Tower.
- **Sater:** convictions in 1991 and 1998, then a decade of documented cooperation with the US government.
- **The deal:** a Trump-signed letter of intent for a Moscow tower carrying large fees.
- **The talks:** with Russian counterparties and Peskov's office, through June 2016, while the candidate said he had no business in Russia.
- **Cohen:** convicted of lying about when the talks ended.
- **SSCI:** its assessment that Putin was almost certainly aware of the deal.

**Does not support:**
- conspiracy or coordination;
- Trump directing Cohen's lie;
- a penthouse offer;
- talks after June 2016;
- Russian tasking of Sater;
- any intelligence chain through Bayrock.

Shared floors and shared deals are proximity, not a chain. The record shows a commercial pursuit that contradicted Trump's public statements.
