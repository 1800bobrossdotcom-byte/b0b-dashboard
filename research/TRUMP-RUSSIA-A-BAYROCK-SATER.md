## WHAT THE DOCUMENTED RECORD SUPPORTS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**Supports:** a decade of Trump–Bayrock/Sater business run out of Trump Tower; Sater's 1991 and 1998 convictions and his documented cooperation with the US government; a Trump-signed LOI for a lucrative Moscow tower; talks with Russian counterparties and Peskov's office through June 2016, while the candidate denied any business in Russia; Cohen's conviction for lying about the end date; SSCI's assessment that Putin was almost certainly aware.

**Does not support:** conspiracy or coordination; Trump directing Cohen's lie; a penthouse offer; talks after June 2016; Russian tasking of Sater; any intelligence chain through Bayrock. Shared floors and deals are proximity. The record shows a commercial pursuit that contradicted Trump's public statements, and nothing beyond it.
