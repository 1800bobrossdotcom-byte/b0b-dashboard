# ZAMPOLLI-A: House Judiciary Democrats' staff report, 26 Oct 2026 (beat A)

Status: HELD research note. Nothing on the page changed. Read 29 Oct 2026. Tiers per CLAUDE.md §3. Women other than Stacey Williams, Maria Farmer and "Katie Johnson" are "Account N". Quotes at the end passed `vq.py` (60/60 OK).

## 1. What it is

- A **53-page unsigned staff report** by the Democratic staff of the House Judiciary Committee, released by Ranking Member Raskin on 26 Oct 2026, eight days before the 3 Nov midterms. PDF sha256 `f48caf3f451224e213b8735de9f1b70d5e82f7d1bbc4ff31654e6fc018bb3582`, 1,468,782 B, text-extracted locally (`pdftext.py`), not from a model summary.
- It is **not a committee finding**: SAN says the Republican majority "hasn't endorsed" it; Newsweek: the claims are "the conclusions of Democratic committee staff, rather than findings by the full House Judiciary Committee or a court". Tier of the report as a document: **documented that it says what it says; every accusation inside it is attributed.**
- Central framing differs by place. Press release: Trump and Paolo Zampolli "may have been co-conspirators". Report's executive summary: Trump "was an active participant". Report's evidence section: Trump "may have participated in" Epstein's crimes (p.34). Its legal standard is the FBI's predication threshold ("may have occurred"), which is a threshold for opening an investigation, not for guilt.
- Recommendations: an independent special counsel (28 CFR 600.1) covering Trump and all Epstein co-conspirators; sharing evidence with New Mexico and the UK; continued congressional oversight. It concedes there is "little reason" to expect Blanche (who appoints special counsels) to act (IBTimes paraphrase; the report says "little reason to believe this Administration will take even this minimal step").

## 4. DOJ-conduct claims vs the transcript

**What the report claims DOJ/Blanche did:**
1. Shut down criminal investigations into enablers from the first weeks of 2025; SDNY files ordered to DC in Jan 2025; July 2025 memo "no further disclosure"; closing of the case declaring no evidence "that could predicate an investigation against uncharged third parties" despite "over 20 alleged co-conspirators from nearly 50 survivors" (all cited to a Raskin letter of 3 Nov 2025, not read here).
2. Withholding/over-redaction under the EFTA (Johnson-linked case documents; a 2009 Goldberger email; FBI handwritten notes; "millions of pages"). The claim that a specific 2009 document is improperly redacted is argued against Blanche's "privileged communication between counsel" explanation.
3. The Maxwell interview: Blanche "directed Maxwell not to discuss ... Jane" on "at least three separate occasions"; this "potentially constituted witness tampering and obstruction" (18 USC 1512, 1505) because Maxwell was due before House Oversight on 11 Aug.
4. Maxwell moved to a minimum-security camp afterwards (Raskin letter, not read).
5. DOJ stalled New Mexico (Zorro Ranch evidence) and a UK MLAT (Raskin letter, 21 Oct 2026, not read).

**Checked at source (justice.gov, no interstitial; real PDFs, 7/24 and 7/25 transcripts, landing page "Updated August 22, 2025"):**
- The three quoted moments are real and verbatim (Day 2 transcript pp.292-293 and 300; the report's footnote dates the Day 2 transcript 24 July, the DOJ file is dated **25 July 2025**; Day 1, 24 July, is the file numbered "07.24").
- **Context the report omits:**
  - p.292: **Blanche raised the topic himself**: "even someone who testified at trial, but certainly have publicly talked about, was as young as 14 when she was introduced to Mr. Epstein." Maxwell then said "I believe you're talking about Jane" and Blanche answered "Yeah." So the report's line that "he did not ask any questions at all about Jane's allegations" is contradicted by his question on p.292 (which asked her to confirm or reject that a minor was brought for a massage). It is accurate only if "Jane's allegations" means the Trump remark.
  - Blanche's interruptions came when Maxwell was **disputing Jane's trial testimony** about Epstein and herself ("I actually don't think that the testimony is correct"; "I'm not doing a he said, she said"). Nothing on the page of the transcript involves Trump or the Trump remark. Whether Blanche knew of the Trump remark is inference (the FBI slide deck, EFTA00164897; his "Yeah"), not in the transcript.
  - p.302: Blanche explains the steering: "I don't think it's helpful for us ... have you kind of address each allegation ... I want you to clear your mind and just tell the truth about it."
  - **Day 1 (pp.104-108): Blanche asked Maxwell directly about Trump**, and she said she never saw him receive a massage, "never witnessed the President in any inappropriate setting in any way", "The President was never inappropriate with anybody", and had never heard anyone say otherwise ("Absolutely never"). The staff report does not mention this. It is the statement of a convicted trafficker with a pending habeas petition (NBC, Spectrum), so it carries no more weight than her denials of her own conduct; but it is the other half of the record the report's "prevented Maxwell from discussing" frame leaves out (symmetry rule).
- Net: **documented** that Blanche said "Let's not talk about individuals" (p.300) and "I don't want to get into ... he said, she said" (p.293) while Maxwell was discussing Jane; **attributed** (report's inference) that this was a design to protect Trump; **not established** that he knew of the Trump remark or intended any effect on her Oversight testimony. Maxwell's House Oversight deposition: subpoena for 11 Aug 2025 (NBC), postponed pending her Supreme Court petition; rescheduled and held **9 Feb 2026**; she invoked the Fifth (TNND/ABC affiliate; Axios refused the spider, null). So the "less than two weeks later" Oversight date never happened as scheduled, which cuts against the idea that her 24-25 July words were being shaped for it.
- Not read: the July 2025 DOJ/FBI memo itself; Raskin's 3 Nov 2025, 9 Nov 2025 and 21 Oct 2026 letters; any EFTA document.

Note the report's counter-document is a 2009 lawyer's email (via a three-party call) recording Trump as saying Epstein "was never asked to leave" and "was not a member"; that email is hearsay of a call, and Trump's own later statements differ (membership, "took people that worked for me"). Tier: dispute of fact between attributed positions; the 2009 email is documented as a document, not as Trump's words.
- **DOJ**: SAN (published early) says DOJ had not commented; IBTimes (27 Oct) quotes a DOJ spokesperson to NBC News calling it "nothing more than a last-ditch effort by House Judiciary Democrats to mislead the public". I found the NBC original only through IBTimes: **attributed, second-hand**.
- **Chairman Jordan / committee Republicans**: nothing found. SAN: the majority has not endorsed. **Null.**
- **Zampolli**: no response to this report found. Earlier public position (Spectrum): he says he organized Melania Knauss's first meeting with Trump, which the report contradicts via an FBI record; Melania Trump denied the Epstein account at a news conference in April 2026 (Spectrum). ## 6. What would PROVE / KILL the central conclusion ("Trump (and Zampolli) may have been co-conspirators"; "active participant")

**Prove (any would move it up a rung; none exists in public now):**
- An unredacted FD-302 or contemporaneous record of Account 4 or Account 8 in which the Trump remark is stated first-hand and consistent with the report's rendering, plus the withheld handwritten notes and the 31E-HQ-C1461253 serials.
- A document (flight manifest with passenger lists, travel records) placing Trump with a named minor and Epstein, independent of the accuser.
- A second, independent first-hand witness to Account 1, 2, 3 or 5's events, recorded before they became public (the report's own corroboration for Account 2 is three people she told later).
- A charging document, plea or sworn testimony naming Trump or Zampolli as a participant; or Maxwell/Brunel/Epstein-estate material doing so.
- A court order compelling release of the withheld Judiciary-requested documents, and what they show.

**Kill (any would substantially weaken it):**
- Intake-form or notes evidence that Account 8 did not describe Trump, or that the location/date errors are the tipster's, or a record that the allegation was investigated and discredited.
- Account 5's earlier lawsuit record (dropped twice) and FBI contact showing the account is not what the report says, or evidence of fabrication.
- Records showing Epstein and Trump were apart on the claimed dates (the 1993 Atlantic City and Plaza pageant for Account 1; the 1993 Mar-a-Lago party for Account 3).
- Release of the Day 1/2 audio or full transcripts that show Blanche's steering was routine proffer practice (partly there already: p.302), or that Jane's remark about Trump was never in his briefing.
- A DOJ/FBI file release (full) containing no Trump-implicating first-hand statement beyond the ones the report cites, so the count of 11 collapses to Accounts 1, 2, 3, 5 (and 8, unverified).
- A court ruling on the 2009 email: if a Mar-a-Lago membership/expulsion record shows Epstein was expelled, the report's "lie either way" dilemma weakens.

## 7. Tiering (my reading, for the author)

- **Documented:** the report's existence, authorship (Democratic staff), content and recommendations; Trump's 2002 "on the younger side" quote, the 2003 birthday-book note (WSJ/AP/House Oversight), flight logs listing Trump (documented that a prosecutor's email says at least eight flights, per Reuters and Newsweek; the logs were not read), the transcript quotes above, Maxwell's Feb 2026 Fifth.
- **Attributed:** each of the five direct accusations (to each accuser, or in Account 8's case to a third-party tip); "Epstein introduced Melania to Trump" (an FBI record of one witness plus another woman's public claim; denied by Melania Trump and Zampolli); the 2009 call summary; "Blanche knew".
- **Contested / not established:** "active participant", "co-conspirator", the cover-up motive, the obstruction theory.
- **Unsupported on this record:** that Blanche acted to shape Maxwell's Oversight testimony.
- **Naming-error candidates in the report:** it merges "Epstein trafficked a woman to a man" with "the man participated" (Accounts 4, 7); it reads "never asked to leave" as an admission that Trump lied while the same email has Trump saying Epstein "was not a member"; it uses "credibly accused" for a civil complaint and "co-conspirator" for people uncharged. Anti-map flag: pageants + models + visas + NYU is a pattern argued across accounts of different strength (the "similarity" paragraph for Accounts 1, 2 and 5 relies on shared themes, some from the accusers' own earlier public tellings).
- **Not for the page:** the individual narratives. If anything goes on the page, it is the document-level fact (the report exists, who wrote it, what it asks for) with the DOJ transcript context, at attributed tier, and the White House denial. Living-person floor: Trump and Zampolli are living and uncharged; both stay "the report alleges". Zampolli's name is the report's; HELD for the author.
