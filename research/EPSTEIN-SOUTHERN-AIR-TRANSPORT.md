# SOUTHERN AIR TRANSPORT, RICKENBACKER, AND THE EPSTEIN CLAIM

**Internal research. `research/` is never served. Nothing here is published.**
Run 15 September 2026, against a claim the author pasted in: that Epstein *"negotiated the
f*cking contract to move the CIA's proprietary airliner, Southern Air Transport"* and *"personally
was the authorized signatory on the deal … to move it to a military base in Columbus, Ohio, to
service the Limited."*

**Verdict in one line: the underlying adjacency is real and documented; the claim as stated
contains a twenty-one-year anachronism that makes its central rhetorical question describe an
event that could not have happened.**

### The IG report's SAT section, in full — this is the primary and it is short

Under the heading **"Allegations Involving Other Companies Associated With the Contras"** — *not*
under proprietaries — the report records exactly two items:

- A **21 Jan 1987 memorandum from ADCI Robert Gates to Morton Abramowitz** stating that US Customs
  had advised CIA that its New Orleans office was investigating an allegation of drug trafficking
  **by SAT crew members**, sourced to a senior FDN official worried that *"scandal emanating from
  Southern Air Transport could redound badly on FDN interests."*
- A **23 Feb 1991 DEA cable** reporting SAT *"of record"* in DEA's database **January 1985 –
  September 1990** for alleged cocaine-trafficking involvement, with an August 1990 entry alleging
  $2m delivered to company sites and *"several of the firm's pilots and executives … suspected of
  smuggling 'narcotics currency.'"*

**That is allegation recorded at crew and executive level. It is adverse and it is real. It is not
a finding that the company was "busted."** The same report's headline conclusion is that *"no
information has been found to indicate that CIA as an organization or its employees conspired
with, or assisted, Contra-related organizations or individuals in drug trafficking."*

## 2. THE THREE THINGS THAT DO NOT SURVIVE

### 2.1 "The CIA's proprietary airliner" — in 1994 there was no proprietary to move

**The CIA sold Southern Air Transport at the end of 1973.** By 1994 it was a privately held company
owned by Bastian, run by William Langton. The counterparties to any Ohio deal were Bastian and
Langton — a private seller of freight capacity chasing a state subsidy package — **not the Central
Intelligence Agency.**

This is not a quibble about phrasing; it is the whole rhetorical engine of the claim. *"How would
you go about in 1994 convincing the Central Intelligence Agency to move its proprietary CIA
airline?"* is a question about an act that had no possible counterparty. **You would not have to
convince the CIA of anything. You would have to outbid Miami, which Ohio did, with a $6m
low-interest loan, a half-million-dollar development grant, and a fifteen-year full property-tax
exemption.** That is the documented mechanism, it is on the record, and it is mundane.

**The article the claim is built on says this itself**, in its third sentence: the agency *"owned
the airline outright from 1960 until 1973, at which point it was sold."* The escalation happened
downstream of the reporting, not in it. In March 1995 Langton told the *Columbus Dispatch* his
company was *"no longer connected to the CIA"* — a denial, worth exactly what a denial is worth,
but the ownership record is independent of it.

### 2.2 "Authorized signatory on the deal" — no document has been produced

The sourcing chain, traced to its end:

> Bob Fitrakis → **David Sturtz**, former Ohio Inspector General, who *"verbally gave me a lot of
> information"* → **Earl Smith**, former Franklin County Sheriff, who per Fitrakis *"knew Epstein
> was the point person in soliciting Southern Air Transport to come to Ohio."*

That is a journalist's recollection of what two former officials told him, about events thirty
years ago, with the underlying files described as destroyed or closed. **Testimony tier: evidence
that these officials said this, never evidence of the transaction.** It is promoted out by a
document and by nothing else.

**No contract, lease, port-authority minute or incentive agreement bearing Epstein's signature has
been produced.** The word *signatory* does not appear anywhere in the Drop Site / *American
Conservative* piece — 98 mentions of Epstein and zero of "signator". **The signature claim is an
escalation added after the reporting, to reporting that does not contain it.**

What *is* documented is the capability, not the act: the **July 1991 power of attorney** gave
Epstein authority to sign for Wexner's affairs. He could have signed a Wexner-side instrument.
Whether he did is exactly the open question, and "he could have" is not "he did."

### 2.3 The Khashoggi bridge is one boast, pointed the wrong way in time

The Adnan Khashoggi link rests on Epstein telling a journalist in **1987** that he had *"found"*
money for Khashoggi — in enough detail that the journalist suspected he *"might be in the business
of hiding as well of finding it."* Take it entirely at face value and it is still (a) a boast by a
man who boasted, (b) about **finding** money, not brokering arms, and (c) dated **after** the
1985–86 arms purchases it is invoked to explain. **A claim that arrives only in the mouth of its
own subject is testimony about the subject's self-presentation.**

## 4. WHAT IS ACTUALLY WORTH HAVING

Strip the Agency out of it and a documented adjacency remains, and it is genuinely interesting:

**An airline whose Contra-era conduct is documented in a CIA Inspector General report relocated,
on public subsidy, into a freight operation serving the retail empire of the man who had given
Jeffrey Epstein his power of attorney four years earlier — and it went bankrupt one week before
that Inspector General report was published.**

Every clause there is sourced. **None of it is a chain.** The anti-map guardrail applies with full
force: a company with a past, a state subsidy, a retail client and a man with signature authority
who was in the state at the time is a shared timeline, not a transaction. The claim converts the
adjacency into an operational tie, and the conversion is the error.

**Symmetry check, running the other way:** none of the above makes SAT clean. The DEA "of record"
window January 1985 – September 1990 is real, adverse and primary. The bankruptcy one week ahead
of publication is a real coincidence and should be recorded as a coincidence rather than smoothed
away. Discounting those because the Epstein claim is overstated would be the same ratchet in
reverse.

**Live-person floor:** Wexner is living and uncharged. The power of attorney is a documented
instrument, not an operational tie. Nothing here asserts otherwise, and the section states the
record and stops.

## 6. A NAMING ERROR NOW CIRCULATING

Several retellings call SAT *"formerly Air America."* **It was not.** Air America and Southern Air
Transport were separate companies held under the same CIA holding structure, the Pacific
Corporation. Collapsing the two is the report's own **naming error** — the sign mistaken for the
thing named — and it is spreading through secondary accounts of this exact story.
