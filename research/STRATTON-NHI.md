**What is genuinely different from Grusch:**
- By Grusch's own account, his 2023 sworn claims came from interviews with others. Stratton claims he saw it himself.
- First-hand sight is stronger testimony of experience. It does not change the tier. Testimony is
  promoted only by a document, never by accumulation, and repeating the claim on a book tour is
  accumulation.

**Symmetry:**
- The book is a financial interest, not a refutation. The page does not discount Ostrovsky or
  Ben-Menashe for writing books, so it does not discount him either.
- Credentials raise the odds that he had access and believes what he says. They say nothing about
  what he saw.

**Guaranteed-null:** "the program is the most classified thing in government" makes silence, denial
and AARO's rebuttal all read as confirmation. Secrecy is evidence of nothing about what is hidden.
Branch B (the documented withholding) stands on its own and needs none of this.

## What would move it

- A document: a program record, a contract line, a special-access-program designation tied to the
  claim.
- Material examined by an outside laboratory.
- Sworn testimony naming a program that an Inspector General or the intelligence committees then
  confirm.
- *Lead, unchecked:* whether the book cleared DoD prepublication review. Clearance would mean
  "not classified as written", not "true".
