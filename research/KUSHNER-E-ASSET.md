# kushner — E (asset: the "Mossad deniable asset" claim)

Researched 26 Oct 2026. Claim tested: **"Jared Kushner is a Mossad deniable asset."** Research for the author only.
Kushner is living and has never been charged. The living-person floor applies: this file records who alleges an
asset role and grades it. It does not adopt it.

**Summary.**
- **Where the claim comes from.** The exact phrase "Mossad deniable asset" appears in no source read. Two sources are
  traceable:
  - Rick Wiles (TruNews), relayed by Raw Story on 8 Mar 2019 from the Daily Beast. He called Kushner a man "working …
    on behalf of his masters in Tel Aviv and Jerusalem". The same broadcast called it "a Jewish mafia operation" and
    named Lewinsky and Barr. That is the dual-loyalty trope, not a source.
  - An FBI Los Angeles **CHS (informant) reporting document** dated Oct 2020. DOJ released it on 30 Jan 2026 (EFTA
    number EFTA00090314, per WION). It relays one informant's belief: "Trump has been compromised by Israel, and Kushner
    is the real brains." The same document, as relayed, also places Kushner in a "Russian-linked organization". It is
    an FBI record **of an allegation**, not an FBI finding. In the same release DOJ called some documents "untrue and
    sensationalist claims" about Trump submitted before the 2020 election. Whether DOJ meant this document is not
    established.
- **Documented.**
  - Netanyahu's long acquaintance with the family, in his own words (15 Feb 2017). The friendship is chiefly the
    father's (already in `KUSHNER-C-PAGE-AUDIT.md` §5).
  - **Kushner led the transition's effort against UNSC Resolution 2334 in Dec 2016** (Mueller Vol. I). Israeli officials
    asked the transition for that help: one senior Israeli official to Reuters, and the WSJ via JTA.
  - The 2018 clearance downgrade, and the 2019 Oversight memos on "Senior White House Official 1" (already in
    `KUSHNER-B-GOVERNMENT.md`).
  - Private Israeli financial institutions in Kushner Companies' deals: a ~$30m Menora Mivtachim investment (2017),
    loans from Bank Hapoalim, a Harel co-investment and a Steinmetz-family partnership. **None of this is Israeli-state
    money.** Hapoalim was state-held only from 1983 to 1996.
- **Attributed.**
  - WaPo, 27 Feb 2018: officials of the **UAE, China, Israel and Mexico** "discussed ways to influence Kushner", per
    US officials familiar with intelligence reports. "It is unclear if any of those countries acted on the discussions."
  - The CHS document's claims.
- **Documented nulls.**
  - Mueller's only Israel-agent inquiry described in Vol. I concerns **Papadopoulos**, not Kushner.
  - Mueller passed counterintelligence material to the FBI in writing, "not all of which is contained in this Volume".
  - No official counterintelligence finding or referral naming Kushner and Israel was found anywhere.
  - The only foreign-agent request on record (Wyden–Raskin, Oct 2024) is about **Saudi Arabia**.
- **Refused:** Israel Hayom, the Times of Israel, Axios, the Senate Judiciary PDF of his 2017 statement (403), and
  WaPo (proxy transport error). The Flynn Statement of Offense URL tried returned 404.

**Ladder verdict:**
- **Rung 2 (documented relationship) with the Israeli government and its prime minister**, reached through his
  official and transition roles and the family friendship.
- **Rung 3 (material relationship)** only with **private** Israeli financial firms, which are not the state and not
  Mossad.
- **Rung 4 is not reached:** no positive record shows him functioning for an Israeli service. On this record the
  service-shaped evidence points as much to the Gulf as to Israel.
- **Rung 5 is claimed for no one.**
