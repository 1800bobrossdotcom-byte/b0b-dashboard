In January 1973 the CIA destroyed its MKUltra records.

Not all of them. More than 20,000 documents had been misfiled in financial records, and the destruction missed them. A FOIA request found them in 1977. "The program's existence was only confirmed because of a filing error."

Hold that. The record you can read is the part that got lost on the way to the fire.

## The clock

Count the years from start to disclosure. MKUltra: 24. Northwoods, the Joint Chiefs' plan for false-flag attacks to justify intervention in Cuba: 39. The Gulf of Tonkin: 41, and an NSA historian's study found the 4 August 1964 attack did not happen. The founding text of the Five Eyes: 64.

The black sites ran on the same shape. The Senate Intelligence Committee found in 2014 that the techniques were brutal and far more extensive than represented, and that the CIA misled Congress, the Justice Department and the White House. A 2009 executive order ordered closure. "The executive order closed the named sites. It did not close the architecture."

## The paper goes first

It is not an American habit. A Colonial Office telegram of 3 May 1961 told officials to keep back papers that "might embarrass Her Majesty's Government". In 1959 the KGB chairman proposed to "destroy all the records concerning the persons shot in 1940" at Katyn.

Britain, Australia, Germany, Israel and India put their intelligence services outside their own access laws, by name or by class. The law that grants the right to know is the law that withholds.

And the money leaves the books by statute. Under the CIA Act of 1949, confidential spending is "accounted for solely on the certificate of the Director and every such certificate shall be deemed a sufficient voucher". The signature is the receipt. In 1974 a taxpayer asked whether that squares with the Constitution's demand for a published account of public money. The Supreme Court held he lacked standing. No court has reached the question since.

On 8 September 2026 New York City published more than 170,000 pages on Ground Zero's air, from 68 boxes, to settle litigation. The city's own account: officials understood the dangers while the public was told the air was safe. Nothing in them was classified. The report has not read the pages, and says so.

The symmetry case is printed too: New Zealand's inquiry into Operation Burnham found the Defence Force misled the public through failures of structure, records and culture, with no organised cover-up. Not every concealment is designed.

And the section kills what it cannot carry. The DIA paid a contractor for reviews titled *Warp Drives* and *Antigravity Studies*: "A government that funds a warp-drive literature review has funded a literature review." Five years of Q drops produced zero documents: "A real insider leaves a trail of documents; Q left a trail of authorship."

## The crime that ran through the banks

In 1981 Italian police raided Licio Gelli's villa and found 962 names: 44 members of parliament, the heads of all three intelligence services, 195 officers. Parliament called the P2 lodge "a secret criminal organization". Gelli, in 2003, on his plan: "I am proud of that."

Gladio was the stay-behind network the CIA and MI6 organised. In 2000 Italy's parliamentary commission concluded the United States had supported the strategy of tension, the years of bombing that took in Piazza Fontana and Bologna, 85 dead at the station. A convicted far-right terrorist gave the purpose: "to force the public to turn to the state to ask for greater security."

In Washington the Kerry Committee found "the supply network of the Contras was used by drug trafficking organizations". Now the popular version: the CIA caused the crack epidemic. The CIA's Inspector General found the Agency failed to act on what it knew, and reported no evidence of a conspiracy to import drugs. Gary Webb's own paper walked back his causal claim as overstated while his core facts stood. He died in 2004 of two gunshot wounds to the head, ruled a suicide; the report notes the anomaly and does not assert homicide.

Then the banks. HSBC moved at least $881 million for the Sinaloa and Norte del Valle cartels, paid a fine worth about five weeks of profit, and no individual was charged. The Attorney General testified some banks were "too big to jail". Wachovia processed $378.4 billion from Mexican exchange houses and paid $160 million.

The report's own audit: "The pattern is real; it is a pattern of documented instances", not one chain run by one set of hands. Whether the fines reflect design, capture or under-resourcing is not established.

## The market with no register

Start with the result nobody wants. Epstein was not the art node. His collection went to a New Jersey auction house, and the trade press told buyers not to expect masterpieces. The viral 935 works worth $2.7 billion trace to a source the report refuses by name.

The mechanism is bigger than him. In July 2020 the Senate's investigators called art "the largest legal, unregulated market in the United States": no Bank Secrecy Act duties at auction, shell-company buyers, advisers for undisclosed principals. Their worked example: two brothers sanctioned by OFAC in 2014 bought more than $18M in art through a Moscow adviser after the sanctions took effect.

The museum was the other use. The Congress for Cultural Freedom was a CIA front in 35 countries; *Encounter*, *Preuves* and *Der Monat* ran on Agency money. Tom Braden went from MoMA's executive secretary to the CIA division that paid for the program. Four of the museum's mid-century leaders held intelligence portfolios: documented biography. That the CIA dictated a style is one historian's thesis, and the report keeps it there.

One killed specimen. *Spirit Cooking*: the email is real, the 1996 artwork is real, and "Every link after that fails on the record." On the island, a released government record shows a room hung with masks of older men, with no identifications; the domed building held a piano and a framed picture of the Pope. The rule: "Aesthetic is an instrument; it is not an act."

## The reach

Here is where this goes past the documents, and I am marking the line. These are b0b.dev's published reaches: readings, not findings.

**Declassification is the state releasing what can no longer cost it.** The same structures run what began around 2013. Project Maven is nine years old; MKUltra was eight in 1961. On the old arithmetic, what surfaces in the 2033–2040 window will be what by then costs nothing.

**Organized crime is the state's off-books department.** The crime funds the covert war, the violence justifies the security budget, the bank keeps the float, and the fine is a licence fee. The documented tier is narrower: separate episodes, each on paper. One loop run by the same hands is the reading, and it is not established.

**Art is the vault and the costume.** Capital hides where no owner is recorded; intelligence wore the museum; a room built to work on people is staging. Ceremony on the island is unsupported, and stays that way.

## What would settle it

**What would prove it:** a release on Maven, or another program begun around 2013, showing its scope was kept from the oversight committees and its records ordered destroyed. A CIA, NATO or SISMI document directing a stay-behind unit or a trafficking channel to act, not merely knowing and failing to act. A CIA or Farfield record showing Agency funding of a museum board after 1967.

**What would kill it:** Maven's full record produced now, matching what Congress was told. The stay-behind files opened whole, showing no service directed, funded or shielded the bombings. Beneficial-ownership records showing the nine-figure buyers and the stored holdings belong to ordinary, declared owners.

## The accident

A misfiled folder, a telegram that outlived its instruction, 68 boxes in a city office. What is missing proves nothing either way. What survived is enough to read.
