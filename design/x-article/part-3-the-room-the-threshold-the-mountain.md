## Under the mountain

In 2006 NORAD announced it was moving primary operations out of Cheyenne Mountain to the surface. By 2015 a $700 million contract was hardening the mountain against electromagnetic pulse, and critical operations went back inside. Beneath the Greenbrier resort, a bunker built for Congress ran from 1958 until a Washington Post investigation exposed it in 1992.

Two claims circulate about all this: that a second civilization has been built underground, and that continuity planning treats the surface as expendable. Neither has a source, and the published documents say the opposite. "The correction is worth more than the claim was."

The real finding is paperwork. Continuity of Government is a line of directives running through eleven administrations of both parties. Its implementing regulation is public, 64 pages. It requires every covered agency to keep a second staff at a separate site, ready within 12 hours and able to hold for 30 days. The operative policy, PPD-40, signed in 2016, has never been published. Which agencies sit in which tier is Annex A, inside the classified directive.
