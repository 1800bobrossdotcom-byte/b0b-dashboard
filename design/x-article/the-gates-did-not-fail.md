## Abroad they answered

The pattern breaks at one border.

London arrested a former Business Secretary on suspicion of misconduct in public office. Oslo made a former Prime Minister a suspect, and his immunity was lifted. Paris opened an investigation into a former Culture Minister.

In Washington, in 2008, the Deputy Attorney General's office "would not intervene". In 2025 a Justice Department memo found nothing that "could predicate an investigation against uncharged third parties."

At home, the same Department holds the files, reads them, and decides whether there is a case. Abroad, the prosecutor is not the custodian of the archive. That difference alone could explain the map of who has answered and who has not.
